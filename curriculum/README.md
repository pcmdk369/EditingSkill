# Complete Master Course Curriculum

## Learning architecture
Eight sequential stages, supported by seven specialist departments. Stages indicate increasing independence and complexity; departments are cross-stage specializations. A learner may study a department selectively after completing its prerequisites.

## Stage 00 — Digital Media Foundations
**Outcome:** Understand digital media, file systems, image/video/audio representation, and safe project setup.

Modules:
1. What editing is; editor roles and production lifecycle
2. Media categories: raster, vector, video, audio, 2D/3D
3. Pixels, dimensions, resolution, aspect ratio and orientation
4. Frame rate, progressive/interlaced scanning, timecode and duration
5. Containers, codecs, compression, bitrate and chroma subsampling
6. Color basics: RGB, HSV/HSL, luma/chroma, bit depth and color spaces
7. Audio: waveform, frequency, sample rate, bit depth, channels and loudness
8. File naming, folders, metadata, proxies and media relinking
9. Storage, backups, versioning and project portability
10. Copyright, consent, accessibility and ethical media use
**Assessment:** Inspect a mixed media pack, identify technical properties, organize it, back it up, and prepare a project.

## Stage 01 — Beginner
**Outcome:** Create simple, coherent edits and export them safely.

Modules:
1. Editing interface, workspaces and project settings
2. Importing, previewing and organizing assets
3. Timeline, tracks, playhead, source monitor and program monitor
4. Selecting, moving, splitting, trimming and deleting clips
5. Insert, overwrite, ripple delete and basic edit operations
6. Basic transitions and continuity
7. Titles, captions, typography and safe areas
8. Image sizing, crop, position and simple transforms
9. Music, voice and basic volume automation
10. Basic exposure, white balance and color correction
11. Export presets, containers, codecs and destination checks
12. First project: 30–60 second story with titles and sound
**Assessment:** Deliver a clean 30–60 second video plus project file and export notes.

## Stage 02 — Intermediate
**Outcome:** Manage structured multi-track projects with deliberate pacing and controlled effects.

Modules:
1. Track hierarchy, nesting, bins and compound sequences
2. Ripple, roll, slip, slide and trim modes
3. J-cuts, L-cuts, match cuts and continuity
4. Keyframes, interpolation and easing
5. Speed changes, slow motion and speed ramps
6. Audio synchronization and dialogue editing
7. Captions, subtitles, transcription and timing
8. Masks, chroma key and simple compositing
9. Stabilization, reframing and aspect-ratio adaptation
10. Proxy workflows and performance management
11. Intermediate color matching and audio cleanup
12. Project: interview, social campaign or short narrative
**Assessment:** Multi-track project with captions, consistent sound, controlled motion, and two aspect-ratio exports.

## Stage 03 — Advanced
**Outcome:** Apply advanced editorial, color, sound, motion and compositing methods.

Modules:
1. Story structure, scene construction and visual grammar
2. Advanced narrative pacing and emotional rhythm
3. Color correction, grading, scopes and shot matching
4. LUTs, color management and display transforms
5. Dialogue cleanup, EQ, compression, dynamics and mixing
6. Sound design, ambience, Foley and effects
7. Motion tracking, planar tracking and object tracking
8. Rotoscoping, keying, masks and compositing
9. Motion graphics, kinetic type and animated data
10. Multi-camera editing and synchronization
11. HDR concepts and delivery considerations
12. Advanced project: commercial, music video or branded film
**Assessment:** Produce a polished 60–120 second piece with documented creative and technical decisions.

## Stage 04 — Expert
**Outcome:** Deliver specialized work to professional briefs and manage client-facing revisions.

Modules:
1. Feature, short-film and documentary editorial methods
2. Advertising, product, corporate and broadcast workflows
3. Music-video and performance editing
4. Interview, news and factual storytelling
5. Advanced continuity, coverage and scene geography
6. Advanced compositing and VFX integration
7. Advanced color science, HDR and finishing
8. Soundscape design and loudness delivery
9. Multi-format, multi-language and platform-specific versions
10. Client briefs, review notes, revisions and approvals
11. Team collaboration, handoffs and project interchange
12. Expert project: client-style production with revision rounds
**Assessment:** Deliver master and platform versions, review exports, change log, and organized handoff.

## Stage 05 — Master
**Outcome:** Lead creative decisions and design reliable high-end post-production pipelines.

Modules:
1. Creative direction and visual language
2. Advanced narrative architecture and audience psychology
3. Editorial strategy for complex productions
4. High-end color pipeline and color-managed finishing
5. Complex VFX, CGI integration and compositing strategy
6. Advanced audio post, immersive formats and mastering concepts
7. Film finishing, conform, online/offline and mastering
8. Large-scale media asset management
9. Production pipeline design, capacity and risk management
10. Quality systems, review protocols and technical delivery specs
11. Leadership, feedback, collaboration and creative problem solving
12. Master capstone: complete short film or campaign package
**Assessment:** Defend creative decisions, deliver a full package, and pass technical/creative review.

## Stage 06 — AI Editing and Automation
**Outcome:** Use AI responsibly to accelerate media analysis and production while retaining human creative control.

Modules:
1. AI media concepts, capabilities and limitations
2. Prompt design for editing, image, audio and video tasks
3. Generative image/video planning and shot continuity
4. Scene, shot, object and speech analysis
5. Transcription, captions, translation and dubbing
6. Voice synthesis, consent and disclosure
7. Background, object and noise removal
8. Upscaling, denoising and frame interpolation
9. AI-assisted rough cuts and highlight selection
10. FFmpeg fundamentals and batch media processing
11. Workflow orchestration, APIs and agent tool design
12. Human approval gates, provenance and output validation
13. AI capstone: repeatable production workflow with QA
**Assessment:** Demonstrate a documented workflow, human review checkpoints, reproducible settings, and validated output.

## Stage 07 — Professional Studio
**Outcome:** Operate an end-to-end production service or studio pipeline.

Modules:
1. Studio roles, briefs, budgets and scope
2. Pre-production research, scripts, storyboards and shot lists
3. Ingest, checksum, backup and asset tracking
4. Editorial scheduling and resource planning
5. Shared storage, collaboration and version control
6. Naming conventions, metadata and media asset management
7. Review, approvals, change requests and client communication
8. Licensing, releases, privacy and legal delivery checks
9. Mastering, QC, packaging, delivery and archive
10. Incident response, recovery and continuity
11. Studio operations, service quality and portfolio
12. Final capstone: end-to-end client simulation
**Assessment:** Submit a complete production package, process documentation, QA report and archive manifest.

## Completion criteria
- Complete prerequisite knowledge checks.
- Pass practical assessments with the published rubric.
- Submit project files and final exports.
- Demonstrate safe asset handling and rights awareness.
- Explain key creative and technical decisions.
- Rework failed criteria before progression.

## Suggested pacing
Self-paced. A stage may take days or months depending on practice time and prior experience. Mastery is assessed by demonstrated work, not time spent.
