# Short-Form Video Workflow

## Brief
Confirm platform, audience, goal, duration, orientation, hook, call to action, caption language, branding and music rights.

## Edit
1. Review source and identify the strongest opening moment.
2. Build a clear hook in the first moments without misleading the audience.
3. Remove dead time while preserving meaning.
4. Use purposeful B-roll, reframing and visual changes.
5. Keep titles and captions readable within platform-safe areas.
6. Balance voice and music; avoid clipping.
7. Add accurate captions and verify names/technical terms.
8. Export to the requested vertical or square profile.
9. Watch the complete export on a phone-sized display.
10. Verify duration, dimensions, sync, text, audio and file integrity.

## Deliverables
Final video, caption file if requested, thumbnail/cover if requested, editable project when supported, and export notes.
