# AI-Assisted Editing Workflow

## Before using AI
Define the task, expected benefit, source rights, privacy constraints, permitted services, budget, and human approval point. Do not upload confidential or personal media to an external service without authorization.

## Workflow
1. Inventory source assets and metadata.
2. Select an AI task: transcription, scene classification, search, cleanup, generation, translation or automation.
3. Choose a tool based on quality, privacy, cost, licensing and export compatibility.
4. Prepare prompts and references; state constraints explicitly.
5. Run a small test before batch processing.
6. Review outputs for hallucinations, artifacts, mistranslation, identity errors and continuity issues.
7. Edit and approve results under human creative control.
8. Record model/tool, settings, source assets and material transformations.
9. Validate final output independently.
10. Disclose synthetic or altered media when required by law, policy, platform or project agreement.
