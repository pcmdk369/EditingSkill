# Universal Production Pipeline

1. **Brief:** purpose, audience, platform, style, duration and success criteria.
2. **Specification:** dimensions, aspect ratio, frame rate, format, audio and caption requirements.
3. **Rights:** ownership, licenses, releases, consent and attribution.
4. **Ingest:** copy media, verify integrity where possible, create asset manifest.
5. **Backup:** preserve source and establish recoverable working copy.
6. **Analysis:** inspect footage, transcripts, images, audio and technical metadata.
7. **Pre-production:** script, storyboard, shot list, selects and timeline plan.
8. **Assembly:** construct the first complete sequence/layout.
9. **Fine edit:** pacing, continuity, timing, typography and transitions.
10. **Finishing:** color, audio, graphics, captions and accessibility.
11. **Export:** use agreed settings and versioned output names.
12. **QC:** validate technical properties and review full playback.
13. **Delivery:** provide deliverables, project files as agreed, notes and manifest.
14. **Archive:** retain approved masters, project assets and documentation under the agreed retention policy.
