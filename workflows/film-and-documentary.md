# Film and Documentary Workflow

1. Read brief, script, interview transcripts and production notes.
2. Organize media by scene, camera, date, interview and audio source.
3. Synchronize sources and verify timecode/audio sync.
4. Create selects and a searchable stringout.
5. Build story structure and assembly.
6. Review factual accuracy, chronology and context.
7. Shape rough cut and fine cut with continuity and pacing.
8. Add B-roll, archival material, graphics and citations/attributions as needed.
9. Complete dialogue edit, sound design, mix and color.
10. Review with stakeholders and log revisions.
11. Conform, caption, export and QC all deliverables.
12. Archive project, source references, releases and final masters.
