# Assessment Framework

## Suggested 100-point practical rubric
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Technical accuracy and specification compliance | 25 |
| Creative quality and visual clarity | 20 |
| Storytelling, structure and pacing | 15 |
| Audio and visual finishing | 15 |
| Workflow, organization and reproducibility | 10 |
| Problem solving and revision quality | 10 |
| Delivery, rights and documentation | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Suggested achievement bands
- 0–49: Not yet competent; targeted remediation required.
- 50–64: Foundation; can complete guided tasks.
- 65–74: Intermediate; can complete structured projects independently.
- 75–84: Advanced; can solve complex production tasks.
- 85–94: Expert; can deliver professional briefs consistently.
- 95–100: Master-level performance on the assessed project.

These are internal learning bands, not external accreditation.

## Evidence
Use project files, final exports, process notes, settings, peer/client feedback, learner explanations and a completed QC report. Assess the work, not just attendance or quiz scores.
