# Photo Editing — EditSkill Specialist Module

## Scope
Develop and retouch photographs while preserving believable detail, tonal range, skin texture and photographic intent.

## Activation and intake

Activate this module for photographic correction, portrait retouching, RAW development, restoration, batch photo consistency or photographic delivery. Coordinate with image-editing for complex composites and with quality-control for independent acceptance review.

Collect the original RAW or highest-quality source, camera/profile metadata where available, intended output and viewing context, reference images, crop constraints, requested retouch level and batch membership. Confirm whether the image is documentary, journalistic, evidentiary, commercial or personal; that context determines which changes could misrepresent reality. Establish subject consent and privacy limits, especially for sensitive portraits or private events. Record whether identity, body shape, skin texture, age indicators, scene details or historical evidence must remain unchanged. Do not infer permission to materially alter a person's appearance from a general request to improve image quality.

## Core principles
Separate global correction from local retouching; protect highlights/shadows; avoid identity-altering edits unless explicitly requested and appropriate; retain original RAW/source files.

## Task procedure
Review exposure, white balance, lens corrections, noise, sharpening and crop; use local masks carefully; synchronize batches only across comparable images.

## Quality-control requirements
Inspect at fit and 100%; check skin/detail, clipping, color consistency, export dimensions, profile, format and metadata.

## Required task record
For substantial work, record the brief, assumptions, source assets, rights/consent notes, selected software and version, key settings, steps performed, output paths, validation evidence and unresolved issues.

## Photography, retouching and restoration workflow

### Source and consent
Record source, camera/scan metadata, ownership, subject/model/property releases, intended use and portfolio permission. Preserve the original capture/scan.

### Procedure
1. Classify task as correction, retouching, restoration or creative manipulation.
2. Record dimensions, bit depth, profile and source condition.
3. Correct exposure/white balance non-destructively.
4. Retouch using layers/masks and preserve texture/detail.
5. Compare before/after at matched crop and scale.
6. For restoration, distinguish preservation master, corrected access copy and interpretive reconstruction.
7. Document AI-generated or reconstructed detail and supporting evidence.
8. Export screen/print versions to explicit specifications.
9. Reopen outputs and inspect color, edges, sharpening and compression.
10. Package original, working file, provenance, consent and delivery manifest.

### QC
Avoid plastic skin, halos, false texture, altered product attributes and unlabeled synthetic historical detail. Never assume image possession equals permission to edit or publish.
