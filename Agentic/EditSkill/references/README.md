## Reference policy
Prefer official software documentation, standards bodies, primary research, and original tool/vendor release notes. Record title, publisher, URL, version/date and access/review date. Distinguish normative standards from recommendations and community guidance. Do not cite a source that has not been checked; do not invent links, editions, compatibility or benchmark results.
