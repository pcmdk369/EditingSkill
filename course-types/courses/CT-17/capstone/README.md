# CT-17 Capstone — Original 2D Animated Short

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 12–20 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Create a 10–15 second original 2D animated sequence with a clear action or story beat. Use a storyboard, animatic, key poses, in-betweens, cleanup/color and a final export.

## Deliverables
- brief, storyboard and animatic;
- character/object model sheet;
- timing charts and key pose breakdown;
- rough animation;
- cleanup and color layers;
- backgrounds/effects where needed;
- final video and image sequence if required;
- AI/provenance record where applicable;
- project manifest, QC report and reflection.

## Mandatory gates
Use original or authorized artwork. Preserve source drawings and disclose/review AI-generated material. No unapproved imitation of protected characters or artists.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Storyboard and staging | 10 |
| Timing/spacing and motion principles | 20 |
| Poses, arcs and mechanics | 20 |
| Drawing/model consistency | 15 |
| Cleanup/color/compositing | 10 |
| Rights and provenance | 10 |
| Technical export/QC | 10 |
| Documentation/reflection | 5 |

Pass: 80/100. Rights and technical playability are mandatory.

## Review
Reviewer checks animatic, frame sequence, model consistency, final playback, source/project and asset rights. Actual learner evidence is required for validation.
