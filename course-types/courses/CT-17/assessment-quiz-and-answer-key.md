# CT-17 — 2D Animation Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. Timing means: A. Duration in frames B. Distance between poses C. Color palette D. File size.
2. Spacing means: A. Movement distance between frames B. Number of tracks C. Frame dimensions D. Sound level.
3. A key pose: A. Defines an important action pose B. Is always the first frame C. Is an export preset D. Is a background.
4. A breakdown pose: A. Defines motion between key poses B. Is a missing frame C. Is a render error D. Is a title card.
5. Onion skin shows: A. Neighboring frames B. Audio levels C. Color profile D. Rights data.
6. A walk cycle includes: A. Contact/down/passing/up B. Only two identical poses C. No weight shift D. Random frames.
7. An animatic tests: A. Story and timing B. Final color grade only C. Audio loudness D. Font license.
8. Cleanup is: A. Refining rough drawings B. Deleting source C. Exporting MP3 D. Applying LUT.
9. AI in-between frames should be: A. Checked for consistency B. Assumed correct C. Used without rights D. Published automatically.
10. An image sequence requires: A. Correct frame numbering and rate B. Random names C. No alpha check D. No manifest.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Explain timing vs spacing.
12. Name three key poses in a jump.
13. Why do feet slide in a walk cycle?
14. What should be reviewed before cleanup begins?
15. Name three quality checks for AI-assisted animation.

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. A character's head changes size across in-betweens and the walk cycle slides. Explain diagnosis and correction. (8)
17. An AI-generated sequence flickers and changes the character's face. What should be done? (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. Timing is duration; spacing is position change between frames. (3)
12. Anticipation, takeoff, apex, landing, recovery (any three). (3)
13. Contact frames and foot-ground timing are inconsistent. (3)
14. Storyboard, animatic, staging, timing and model approval. (3)
15. Frame consistency, anatomy/model, flicker, rights/provenance. (3)
16. Compare model sheet/onion skin, correct volume in key/breakdown frames, fix contact pose and spacing, preview full loop. (8)
17. Reject or manually correct sequence; restore approved model, inspect frame by frame, document AI provenance and do not deliver until consistent. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Rights and final sequence integrity are mandatory.
