# CT-11 Capstone — Documented Historical Photo Restoration Collection

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–16 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Restore a collection of three authorized damaged photographs. Preserve untouched originals and document repair, reconstruction, uncertainty, rights and archive decisions.

## Deliverables
- source/provenance register;
- untouched scans and metadata;
- damage maps;
- three layered restorations;
- preservation masters and access copies;
- reference and reconstruction log;
- AI use/provenance note if applicable;
- intervention/disclosure statement;
- rights/access restrictions;
- QC report and archive manifest;
- reflection explaining uncertainty and restraint.

## Mandatory gates
No fabricated historical detail presented as fact. Preserve originals, document synthetic/reconstructed areas and respect privacy/rights.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Provenance and source preservation | 15 |
| Damage assessment | 10 |
| Repair craft | 20 |
| Historical integrity and uncertainty | 20 |
| Non-destructive workflow | 10 |
| Disclosure and ethics | 10 |
| Archive and manifest | 10 |
| QC/reflection | 5 |

Pass: 80/100. Source integrity and truthful disclosure are mandatory gates.

## Review
Reviewer checks original preservation, restoration artifacts, reconstruction provenance, rights and archive integrity. Actual learner files and review evidence are required for validation.
