# CT-11 — Photo Restoration & Manipulation Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. A restoration workflow should preserve: A. Untouched original B. Only JPEG C. Only screenshot D. No source.
2. Provenance describes: A. Image origin/history B. Export size C. Font choice D. Frame rate.
3. Clone tool: A. Copies pixels B. Blends automatically C. Adds audio D. Grants rights.
4. Healing tool: A. Blends sampled content with surroundings B. Changes color profile only C. Creates vector paths D. Removes all noise.
5. Missing unique historical details should be: A. Invented B. Documented as uncertain C. Guessed D. Hidden.
6. Colorization is: A. Adding color to monochrome source B. Recovering original color always C. Exporting TIFF D. Cropping.
7. AI upscaling may: A. Synthesize details B. Prove identity C. Recover all lost facts D. Establish provenance.
8. Preservation master and access copy are: A. Distinct archive/delivery roles B. Always same file C. Both temporary D. Unnecessary.
9. Intervention log should include: A. Tools/operations/uncertainty B. Only file name C. Likes D. No edits.
10. Public sharing requires: A. Rights/privacy review B. No permission C. Only higher resolution D. Automatic approval.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Why should the original scan remain untouched?
12. Name three common damage types.
13. Why is AI enhancement not proof recovery?
14. What is the difference between restoration and creative manipulation?
15. What information belongs in a restoration archive manifest?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. An AI tool reconstructs a missing inscription on an old photograph. The wording looks plausible but no reference exists. Explain what to do. (8)
17. A restored image looks oversaturated compared with the source and no color reference exists. Describe a responsible decision. (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 B; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. It preserves evidence and enables future reinterpretation. (3)
12. Dust, scratches, tears, fading, stains, missing regions. (3)
13. Models may invent plausible pixels not present in source. (3)
14. Restoration repairs with evidence; manipulation may alter meaning or content. (3)
15. File ID, source/provenance, format/profile, intervention history, rights/access and checksums where used. (3)
16. Reject as historical fact; retain source, leave area unreconstructed or label clearly as speculative interpretation; record AI tool and uncertainty. (8)
17. Reduce unsupported saturation, preserve monochrome or present colorization as interpretive, clearly labeled; do not claim historical accuracy. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Fabricated evidence or unauthorized use requires correction.
