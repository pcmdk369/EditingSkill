# CT-07 Capstone — Professional Five-Image Editorial or Product Series

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–14 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Produce a coherent five-image series for one defined purpose: editorial portrait, product catalog, event story or creative campaign. Document source, rights, retouch scope, color decisions and output specifications.

## Deliverables
- brief and visual reference board;
- source inventory and rights/releases;
- five edited images;
- layered editable project or documented RAW workflow;
- before/after review images;
- color/profile/export specification;
- retouch and provenance log;
- QC report and delivery manifest;
- 500-word reflection and portfolio case study draft.

## Mandatory criteria
Preserve originals. Do not invent product properties or misrepresent documentary evidence. Respect model/property releases and image licenses. Consider metadata privacy. Obtain portfolio permission where required.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Brief and art direction | 10 |
| Technical correction | 15 |
| Selection/mask/layer workflow | 15 |
| Retouch/compositing quality | 15 |
| Series consistency | 15 |
| Rights, consent and integrity | 10 |
| Export compliance and QC | 10 |
| Documentation and handoff | 10 |

Pass 80/100; rights, source preservation and factual integrity are mandatory gates.

## Review
Reviewer checks originals, layered project, all exports, profile/dimensions, retouch ethics and manifest. Validation requires actual learner artifacts and review evidence.
