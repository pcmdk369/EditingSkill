# CT-07 — Photo & Image Editing Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. A raster image is made of: A. Pixels B. Audio samples C. Vector paths only D. Frames per second.
2. A mask controls: A. Visibility B. Copyright C. File name D. Sample rate.
3. A non-destructive workflow: A. Deletes source B. Preserves editable/reversible changes C. Flattens immediately D. Resaves JPEG repeatedly.
4. A histogram shows: A. Tonal distribution B. Exact aesthetic quality C. Rights status D. Lens model.
5. White balance is used to: A. Correct color cast B. Add resolution C. Change file owner D. Remove license.
6. Batch presets: A. Never need review B. Need per-image review C. Guarantee identical light D. Replace source.
7. A composite should match: A. Perspective and light B. File size only C. Social likes D. Font license only.
8. Retouching boundaries depend on: A. Context and brief B. Tool default C. Export codec D. Image name.
9. AI generative fill can: A. Invent details B. Prove facts C. Grant consent D. Guarantee accuracy.
10. Print/web output format should be chosen by: A. Destination requirements B. Random choice C. Default only D. Camera brand.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Explain the difference between resolution and color profile.
12. Name three advantages of layers and masks.
13. Why should a batch preset be treated as a starting point?
14. Give two examples of an alteration that could mislead a viewer.
15. What belongs in an image-delivery manifest?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. A product image is retouched with generative fill. The tool invents a new label and changes a connector shape. Describe the correction, provenance and review process. (8)
17. Five images look different after export on another device. Describe a color-management diagnosis. (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 B; 4 A; 5 A; 6 B; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. Resolution is pixel dimensions/density in context; profile describes interpretation of color values. (3)
12. Reversibility, local control, independent adjustments, easier revisions. (3)
13. Source lighting and subject differences need individual correction. (3)
14. Altering product feature/condition, removing safety label, changing documentary evidence. (3)
15. Filename, dimensions, format, profile, version, rights/usage and approval status. (3)
16. Reject or replace invented areas with verified source assets, compare to real product, retain original, log tool/model/date and corrections, obtain approval. (8)
17. Check embedded/assigned profiles, export conversion, monitor calibration/viewing conditions, destination profile and file metadata; create a proof and compare under consistent conditions. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Deceptive factual alteration or rights violation requires correction.
