# 3D Animation

**Course ID:** CT-18  
**Pathway:** Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master → AI & Automation → Professional Practice

## Purpose and outcomes
Develop 3D animation skills from scene setup and transforms through interpolation, character rigging, simulation, rendering, shot direction, responsible motion-capture assistance and professional delivery.

Learners will animate objects/characters, manage rigs and caches, optimize renders, review AI-assisted motion and package reproducible scenes.

## Software track
Use a current 3D application with animation, rigging, simulation and rendering. Record version, renderer, scene units, frame rate and hardware. Verify exact steps against official documentation.

## Completion requirements
Pass the 50-point assessment, submit lab and capstone, meet rights/rig/render gates and provide a reproducible scene package. Suggested capstone pass: 80/100. Independent review and learner execution evidence remain required.
