# AI-Assisted Image Editing

**Course ID:** CT-24  
**Pathway:** Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master → AI & Automation → Professional Practice

## Purpose and outcomes
Develop responsible AI-assisted image workflows from task selection and source rights through inpainting, upscaling, compositing, batch QA, art direction, provider evaluation and professional delivery.

Learners will choose appropriate tasks, preserve originals, control regions, detect synthetic errors, document provenance and deliver reviewed outputs.

## Tool/provider track
Select providers only after reviewing current terms, privacy, retention, training use and commercial rights. Use conventional raster editing for precision and final corrections. Preserve originals and document generated regions.

## Completion requirements
Pass the 50-point assessment, submit lab and capstone, meet rights/privacy/factual gates and provide provenance records. Suggested capstone pass: 80/100. Independent review and learner execution evidence remain required.
