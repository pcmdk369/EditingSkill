# CT-09 Capstone — Professional Portrait Retouch Package

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–14 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Produce a natural, consistent three-image portrait package for an authorized subject. The subject's identity and agreed features must remain respected. Define retouch scope and privacy handling before editing.

## Deliverables
- brief and retouch policy;
- subject consent and usage record;
- original/source inventory;
- three layered retouched portraits;
- web/high-resolution exports;
- before/after proofs;
- retouch and AI provenance log;
- privacy/metadata note;
- QC report and delivery manifest;
- reflection and portfolio authorization status.

## Mandatory gates
No unauthorized external upload, identity-misrepresenting edits or public display without permission. Preserve original files and follow retention requirements.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Scope, consent and source protection | 15 |
| Natural skin retouch | 20 |
| Light/color consistency | 15 |
| Hair/edge detail | 10 |
| Individual subject fidelity | 15 |
| Non-destructive project quality | 10 |
| Privacy, export and delivery | 10 |
| Documentation/reflection | 5 |

Pass 80/100; consent and identity integrity are mandatory gates.

## Review
Reviewer compares originals and finals, inspects texture/edges/color, verifies consent and metadata handling, and checks package integrity. Validation requires actual learner outputs and documented review.
