# CT-09 Professional Lab 02 — Portrait Retouching, Consent and Authenticity Review

- Course: CT-09 — Portrait & Beauty Retouching
- Level: Advanced / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 3–5 hours
- Environment: Raster editor with layers/masks, color-managed workflow where available, secure asset storage
- Lab status: Authored procedure; not executed or independently reviewed

## 1. Brief
Create two portrait deliverables from an authorized subject photograph: a natural editorial correction and a clearly disclosed creative/beauty treatment. Preserve the source, document requested changes and avoid edits that misrepresent identity, age, body characteristics or product results.

## 2. Learning objectives
- distinguish technical correction from subjective retouching and identity-altering manipulation;
- obtain and respect subject/client instructions and consent;
- use non-destructive retouching;
- preserve skin texture, lighting and realistic detail;
- create a traceable before/after and approval workflow.

## 3. Inputs and consent
Use learner-created images or images with documented permission for editing and intended distribution. Confirm whether the subject has approved retouching scope, portfolio display and any public before/after comparison. These permissions may be separate. Do not infer consent from possession of an image.

## 4. Retouching categories
| Category | Examples | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Technical correction | Exposure, white balance, lens dust | Usually reversible and documented |
| Temporary cleanup | Sensor spots, temporary blemish if requested | Preserve natural texture |
| Styling/creative | Color grade, background treatment | Match brief and disclose when material |
| Identity/body alteration | Face shape, body size, age cues | Requires explicit scope and careful review; may be inappropriate |
| Deceptive representation | Altering evidence, results or protected identity | Do not perform for misleading use |

## 5. Procedure
1. Verify subject identity only from the provided project record; do not infer sensitive traits.
2. Confirm edit scope, intended use, consent and portfolio permission.
3. Preserve the original and create a separate working project.
4. Record source dimensions, color profile and delivery requirements.
5. Apply global exposure and color corrections first.
6. Use separate layers/masks for cleanup, tone and creative treatment.
7. Work at 100% for artifacts and at delivery size for overall impression.
8. Preserve realistic skin texture and natural transitions unless a clearly defined creative brief requires otherwise.
9. Compare before/after at matched crop and display scale.
10. Ask the subject/client to review the requested changes before final approval.
11. Record edits, reviewer feedback and final approval.
12. Export only the approved version and keep the original and project package secure.

## 6. Validation
| Test | Method | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Consent | Inspect authorization and intended use | Scope covers edit and delivery |
| Source preservation | Compare original | Unchanged |
| Texture | Inspect at 100% | No unintended plastic texture/artifacts |
| Anatomy/edges | Inspect masks and geometry | No accidental distortion |
| Color | Compare under consistent viewing | Natural or brief-approved result |
| Before/after | Matched crop and scale | Changes are transparent |
| Approval | Review record | Correct version approved |
| Portfolio | Separate permission | No public use without permission |

## 7. Troubleshooting
- Plastic skin: reduce blur/frequency separation and restore texture.
- Halo around hair: refine mask edge and inspect against contrasting backgrounds.
- Uneven skin tone: use subtle local adjustments rather than global desaturation.
- Unrequested face/body change: revert and seek explicit direction; do not assume preference.
- Approval mismatch: stop delivery and compare exact version to approved proof.

## 8. Deliverables
Original inventory, consent/use record, layered project, natural editorial version, creative version where approved, before/after proof, edit log, approval record and export manifest.

## 9. Assessment — 100 points
Consent/scope 20; non-destructive workflow 15; technical correction 15; natural detail 15; edge/anatomy quality 10; transparency/approval 15; delivery/security 10. Pass 80/100. Unauthorized image use or deceptive representation is a critical failure.

## 10. Completion checklist
- [ ] Editing and distribution permission verified.
- [ ] Portfolio permission separately checked.
- [ ] Original preserved.
- [ ] Retouching layers remain editable.
- [ ] Subject/identity not altered beyond approved scope.
- [ ] Before/after shown at matched scale.
- [ ] Final version approved and traceable.
- [ ] Delivery package stored securely.
