# CT-09 Professional Lab 01 — Natural Portrait Retouch and Subject Review

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 4–6 hours
- Environment: Raster editor/RAW processor; record OS, app/version and display profile
- Lab status: Authored procedure; not executed in this session

## Brief
Retouch a three-image portrait set for a professional profile package. Preserve natural skin texture and identity features, follow an agreed scope and deliver approved crops securely.

## Prerequisites and rights
Complete CT-09 Lessons 01–04. Use learner-created or properly licensed portraits with subject consent. Do not use identity documents or sensitive third-party portraits as test material.

## Procedure
1. Record subject consent, usage scope and retouch boundaries.
2. Preserve originals and create a working copy.
3. Inspect focus, exposure, white balance and crop.
4. Correct global exposure/color before local retouching.
5. Clean temporary blemishes on a separate layer.
6. Preserve skin texture, facial structure and distinguishing features.
7. Use restrained dodge/burn for lighting continuity.
8. Refine hair edges and inspect against light/dark backgrounds.
9. Compare all three portraits for consistent tone without forcing identical complexion.
10. Export proofs and obtain subject/client review where agreed.
11. Apply approved revisions and export final formats.
12. Inspect metadata, filenames and secure delivery package.

## Expected artifacts
Three layered portrait edits, before/after proofs, consent/scope record, retouch log, final crops, QC report and manifest.

## Validation
| Test | Method | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Naturalness | Compare at 100% and output size | Texture and identity preserved |
| Color | Compare set and neutral references | No unintended skin cast |
| Edges | Inspect hair against contrasting backgrounds | No visible halo |
| Scope | Compare edits to approved brief | No unauthorized changes |
| Privacy | Inspect metadata/storage | Sensitive information protected |
| Delivery | Open all exports | Correct crops and formats |

## Troubleshooting
Plastic skin: reduce smoothing and restore texture. Halo: refine mask. Skin/neck mismatch: correct color locally. Unapproved alteration: restore source and seek approval. Privacy issue: stop transfer and follow incident procedure.

## Assessment — 100 points
Consent/scope 15; natural retouch 25; skin tone/light 15; hair/detail 10; consistency 10; non-destructive source 10; privacy/delivery 10; documentation 5. Pass 80/100; consent and identity integrity are mandatory gates.

## Handoff
Submit project, exports, consent record, retouch log, QC evidence and delivery manifest.
