# Motion Graphics & Kinetic Typography

**Course ID:** CT-19  
**Pathway:** Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master → AI & Automation → Professional Practice

## Purpose and outcomes
Develop motion-graphics and kinetic-typography skills from design hierarchy and timeline setup through shape animation, data motion, reusable systems, art direction, responsible AI and professional delivery.

Learners will create readable motion, animate typography and shapes, build responsive systems, preserve data accuracy and deliver reusable project packages.

## Software track
Use a current motion graphics/compositing application with text, masks, keyframes and export. Record version, fonts, plugins, frame rate and alpha requirements. Verify exact application-specific instructions.

Pass the 50-point assessment, submit lab and capstone, meet text/data/rights/accessibility gates and deliver editable source with dependencies. Suggested capstone pass: 80/100. Independent review and learner execution evidence remain required.
