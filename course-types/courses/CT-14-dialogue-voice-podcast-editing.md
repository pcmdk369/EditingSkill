# Dialogue, Voice & Podcast Editing

**Course ID:** CT-14  
**Pathway:** Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master → AI & Automation → Professional Practice

## Purpose and outcomes
Develop professional speech-editing and podcast-production skills from recording/session setup through dialogue pacing, voice processing, multitrack sync, loudness, series direction, responsible AI and delivery.

Learners will preserve speaker meaning, edit natural dialogue, improve intelligibility, synchronize remote guests, review transcripts, protect consent and deliver reproducible sessions.

Pass the 50-point assessment, submit the lab and capstone, meet consent/rights/meaning-preservation gates and deliver a reproducible session package. Suggested capstone pass: 80/100. Independent review and learner execution evidence remain required.
