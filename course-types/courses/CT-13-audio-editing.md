# Audio Editing

**Course ID:** CT-13  
**Pathway:** Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master → AI & Automation → Professional Practice

## Purpose and outcomes
Develop audio-editing competence from source inspection and session setup through dialogue cleanup, EQ/dynamics, routing, loudness validation, mix direction, responsible AI assistance and professional delivery.

Learners will preserve originals, edit cleanly, process audio conservatively, synchronize sound, measure final exports and deliver editable sessions/stems with rights documentation.

## Software track
Use a current DAW/audio editor with waveform editing, processing, routing and export measurement. Record application/version, interface and monitoring assumptions. Use the delivery brief for loudness/true-peak targets; no universal platform target is assumed.

## Completion requirements
Pass the 50-point assessment, submit the lab and capstone, meet rights/sync/technical gates and provide a reproducible session handoff. Suggested capstone pass: 80/100. Independent review and learner execution evidence remain required.
