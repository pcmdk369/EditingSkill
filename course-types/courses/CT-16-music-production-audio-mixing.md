# Music Production & Audio Mixing

**Course ID:** CT-16  
**Pathway:** Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master → AI & Automation → Professional Practice

## Purpose and outcomes
Develop music production skills from DAW setup and MIDI arrangement through recording/comping, mixing, mastering, production direction, responsible AI use and professional delivery.

Learners will organize sessions, arrange and record music, mix with controlled processing, meet delivery targets, manage rights and provide validated stems and project archives.

## Software track
Use a current DAW with MIDI, recording, mixing and export capability. Record sample rate, bit depth, plugin dependencies and monitoring assumptions. Verify all sample/voice rights and delivery specifications.

## Completion requirements
Pass the 50-point assessment, submit lab and capstone, meet rights and technical delivery gates, and provide validated stems/session. Suggested capstone pass: 80/100. Independent review and learner execution evidence remain required.
