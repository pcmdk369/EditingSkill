# CT-18 Capstone — Original 3D Character Animation Shot

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 12–20 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Create a 5–10 second 3D character animation shot with readable staging, a clear action, camera, lighting and final render. Use an original or authorized character/rig.

## Deliverables
- brief and shot plan;
- character model/rig and source assets;
- key poses and animation curves;
- playblast and review notes;
- final render or image sequence;
- scene/render settings;
- texture/cache/dependency manifest;
- AI/motion-capture consent/provenance where applicable;
- QC report and reflection.

## Mandatory gates
Use authorized character and motion-capture assets. Preserve editable scene and verify final sequence, color and dependencies.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Story/staging and camera | 10 |
| Character rig/deformation | 15 |
| Animation timing/performance | 20 |
| Motion arcs and continuity | 10 |
| Lighting/material/render | 15 |
| Pipeline/dependencies | 10 |
| Rights/provenance | 5 |
| Technical QC and handoff | 10 |
| Reflection | 5 |

Pass: 80/100. Rights, render playability and source package are mandatory.

## Review
Reviewer inspects scene, rig, playblast, final sequence, dependencies and rights. Actual learner artifacts and review evidence are required for validation.
