# Photo Restoration & Manipulation

**Course ID:** CT-11  
**Pathway:** Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master → AI & Automation → Professional Practice

## Purpose and outcomes
Develop evidence-aware restoration and manipulation skills while preserving originals, provenance, historical context and transparent disclosure.

Learners will assess damage, repair scans, recover tone/color cautiously, reconstruct missing areas from evidence, direct collection standards, evaluate AI enhancement and deliver traceable preservation archives.

## Software track
Use a current raster editor and suitable scan/RAW workflow. Record scan resolution, bit depth, profile and software version. Preserve source scans and separate preservation masters from access copies.

## Completion requirements
Pass the 50-point assessment, submit lab and capstone, preserve originals, document reconstruction and meet rights/privacy gates. Suggested capstone pass: 80/100. Independent review and learner execution evidence are required.
