# CT-16 Capstone — Original Music Cue Production and Delivery

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 12–18 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Produce an original 60–120 second music cue for a defined visual or listening context. Create an arrangement, record/program parts, mix and master against a supplied delivery specification.

## Deliverables
- production brief and reference palette;
- editable DAW session and source media/MIDI;
- arrangement map and track/bus diagram;
- mix and processing notes;
- stereo master and alternate mix;
- aligned stems;
- sample/performer/composition rights register;
- AI use/provenance record if applicable;
- loudness/true-peak report;
- QC report and archive manifest;
- reflection and portfolio permission status.

## Mandatory gates
No unlicensed samples or unauthorized voice imitation. Preserve original takes and verify final rendered outputs.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Brief and arrangement | 15 |
| Performance/MIDI craft | 10 |
| Mix balance and processing | 20 |
| Mastering/delivery compliance | 15 |
| Stem accuracy and alignment | 15 |
| Rights/provenance | 10 |
| Session/archive quality | 5 |
| QC/reflection | 10 |

Pass: 80/100. Rights and delivery compliance are mandatory.

## Review
Reviewer listens to full master/alternates, measures outputs, recombines stems and checks session/rights records. Actual learner files and review evidence are required.
