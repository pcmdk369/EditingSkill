# CT-16 Professional Lab 01 — Arrangement, Mix, Master and Stem Delivery

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 5–7 hours
- Environment: DAW and audio interface; record OS/app/version, sample rate and monitoring
- Lab status: Authored procedure; not executed in this session

## Brief
Produce a 60-second original music cue from MIDI and/or recorded audio. Deliver a stereo master, instrumental alternate and aligned stems against a supplied specification.

## Prerequisites
Complete CT-16 Lessons 01–05. Use original or licensed samples, instruments and recordings. Record collaborator and sample rights.

## Procedure
1. Create session and record tempo, meter, sample rate and bit depth.
2. Organize tracks, folders and buses.
3. Build a 16–32 bar arrangement with clear sections.
4. Record or program parts; preserve raw takes/MIDI.
5. Edit and comp performances with natural timing.
6. Establish static mix balance and gain staging.
7. Apply EQ, compression, reverb and automation only as needed.
8. Check phase, mono compatibility and headroom.
9. Obtain delivery loudness/true-peak target from brief.
10. Master and export a proof; measure the rendered file.
11. Export master, alternate and stems with common start/end.
12. Recombine stems to validate.
13. Complete rights, version and delivery manifest.

## Expected artifacts
Editable DAW session, original MIDI/audio, stereo master, alternate mix, stems, rights register, loudness report and QC manifest.

## Validation
| Test | Method | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Arrangement | Full listen | Musical structure coherent |
| Mix | Reference and alternate playback | Clear balance, no clipping |
| Master | Meter final render | Meets supplied target |
| Phase | Mono check | No unacceptable cancellation |
| Stems | Recombine | Reconstructs approved mix |
| Rights | Asset audit | All sources authorized |
| Handoff | Reopen project | Dependencies documented |

## Troubleshooting
Clipping: reduce gain before master. Muddy mix: identify masking. Loudness out of target: revise and remeasure. Missing stem effect: inspect routing. Sample rights unclear: replace or obtain license.

## Assessment — 100 points
Arrangement 15; recording/MIDI craft 10; mix 20; mastering 15; stem integrity 15; rights 10; session organization 5; QC/handoff 10. Pass 80/100; rights and rendered-file validation are mandatory.

## Handoff
Submit session, master, alternate, stems, rights log, meter report, version manifest and QC notes.
