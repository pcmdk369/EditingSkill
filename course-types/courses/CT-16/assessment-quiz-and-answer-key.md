## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. MIDI stores note/control events; audio stores sound samples. (3)
12. It establishes relationships before processing. (3)
13. Phase/mono, masking, clipping, translation, stereo image. (3)
14. Estimate of peak level after waveform reconstruction. (3)
15. Master/alternate formats, stems, session, sample/rights, version, measurements and dependencies. (3)
16. Return to mix, restore headroom/dynamics, reduce limiting, measure true peak and loudness on rendered proof, then export final. (8)
17. Check common start/end, routing, bus effects, automation, pan/channel layout and stem export settings. (7)
