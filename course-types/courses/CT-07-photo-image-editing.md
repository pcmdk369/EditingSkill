# Photo & Image Editing

**Course ID:** CT-07  
**Pathway:** Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master → AI & Automation → Professional Practice

## Purpose and outcomes
Develop professional image-editing skills across file preparation, non-destructive correction, selection/masking, color management, retouching, batch consistency, art direction, responsible AI use and delivery.

Learners will preserve originals, edit with layers/masks, correct tone/color, produce ethical composites, process image sets, direct a coherent series, review AI alterations and deliver traceable image packages.

Use a current raster editor and RAW processor as appropriate. Record app/version, OS, color-management assumptions and destination profile. Software-specific UI procedures must be verified against official documentation.

## Completion requirements
Pass the 50-point assessment, submit the lab and capstone, preserve originals, meet rights/factual integrity gates and provide a documented delivery package. Suggested capstone pass: 80/100. Independent review and learner execution evidence are required for validation.
