# CT-01 Video Editing — Capstone Asset and Assessment Pack

**Status:** Authored framework; learner-created or licensed media required.  
**Course:** CT-01 — Video Editing  
**Owner:** PCMDK369™

## 1. Capstone brief
Create a 60–90 second story in both 16:9 landscape and 9:16 vertical formats. The story may document a simple process, introduce a product, explain a useful idea or present a short human-interest moment. Use original, licensed or explicitly authorized media only.

## 2. Required source pack
Learner supplies:
- 8–15 video clips covering wide, medium, close-up and detail shots;
- 1–3 minutes of dialogue or narration if the story needs speech;
- room tone or relevant natural sound;
- licensed/original music only if appropriate;
- title/credit text and any approved graphic assets;
- a written brief and delivery specification.

Do not distribute copyrighted sample footage, commercial music or identifiable private recordings as course assets without documented permission. Instructors may provide self-created synthetic practice files.

## 3. Deliverables
1. Project folder with editable project and organized media.
2. Media inventory and rights/consent record.
3. Story outline or edit decision list.
4. Landscape master and vertical adaptation.
5. Captions/subtitles when speech or accessibility requirements call for them.
6. Export metadata record and QC checklist.
7. Short reflection describing three editorial decisions and one limitation.
