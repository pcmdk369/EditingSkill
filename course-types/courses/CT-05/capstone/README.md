# CT-05 Capstone — Commercial Campaign Master and Cutdown Suite

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 12–18 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Scenario
Create a campaign for a fictional product or a real product with permission. Deliver a 30-second master and 15-/6-second cutdowns. Include one vertical and one landscape adaptation if the brief requires both.

## Deliverables
- approved campaign brief and brand guide summary;
- storyboard and shot/evidence matrix;
- claim ledger and substantiation references;
- asset, music, talent and location rights log;
- editable master and derivative sequences;
- 30-, 15- and 6-second exports;
- captions/disclaimers as required;
- approval/change log;
- QC report and delivery manifest;
- 500-word reflection and portfolio case study draft.

## Required gates
No fabricated testimonials, unsupported product claims, false product imagery, unlicensed audio or unauthorized talent. Jurisdiction-specific review must be completed by the responsible client/legal authority. Preserve original project and distinguish review from approved files.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Brief, audience and creative strategy | 10 |
| Story and visual hierarchy | 15 |
| Product proof and claim integrity | 20 |
| Cutdown adaptation | 15 |
| Brand, sound and accessibility | 10 |
| Rights and approval governance | 15 |
| Technical QC and delivery | 10 |
| Reflection and portfolio accuracy | 5 |

Pass: 80/100. Rights, factual claims and correct approved version are mandatory gates.

## Review
Reviewer checks each version against brief, claim ledger and destination specs, watches every export and records defects. Completion requires evidence from actual learner outputs; this brief is not proof of execution.
