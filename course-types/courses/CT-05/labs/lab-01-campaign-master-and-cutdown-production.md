# CT-05 Professional Lab 01 — Campaign Master and 6/15/30-Second Cutdowns

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 5–7 hours
- Environment: NLE with multiple sequences and export presets; record OS, editor/version and hardware
- Lab status: Authored procedure; not executed or tool-tested in this session

## 1. Brief
Create one approved campaign master and 6-, 15- and 30-second versions for a fictional consumer product. Every version must preserve accurate product representation, required qualifiers and a clear CTA.

## 2. Objectives
Build a claim ledger, storyboard, master sequence, three cutdowns, approval record, export manifest and QC report.

## 3. Prerequisites and setup
Complete CT-05 Lessons 01–05. Use learner-created or licensed footage, approved brand assets and original/licensed audio. Confirm the campaign market and applicable review authority. This lab is not legal advice.

## 4. Inputs and rights
Source footage, product fact sheet, brand guide, CTA, approved offer/price, music license, talent releases and destination specifications. Record permitted media, territory, term and usage restrictions where supplied.

## 5. Structure
```text
ct05-campaign/
  brief/

```

## 6. Implementation
1. Translate brief into audience, proposition, proof and CTA.
2. Build a claim ledger; flag price, comparison, guarantee and performance claims for approval.
3. Create a shot/evidence matrix and storyboard.
4. Assemble a master edit with all necessary context and proof.
5. Duplicate sequences for 6-, 15- and 30-second versions.
6. Rebuild the narrative for each runtime; do not merely accelerate the master.
7. Reframe graphics and captions for each aspect ratio.
8. Check music/voice rights and dialogue intelligibility.
9. Verify required disclaimers and qualifications remain readable.
10. Export review copies and obtain authorized approval.
11. Export approved versions and watch each from beginning to end.
12. Complete a manifest with file properties and usage notes.

## 7. Expected artifacts
Master and three cutdowns, editable project, storyboard, claim ledger, rights log, approval matrix, manifest and QC report.

## 8. Validation
| Test | Method | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Claims | Compare all versions to ledger | Approved wording and evidence |
| Duration | Inspect metadata/playback | Meets each brief |
| Brand | Compare with guide | Correct assets and hierarchy |
| Audio | Full playback | Intelligible, licensed, no clipping |
| Legal copy | Review at actual display size | Readable and approved |
| Rights | Inspect licenses/releases | Use within granted scope |
| Version | Compare manifest | Exact file/version identification |

## 9. Troubleshooting and rollback
If a cutdown removes a qualifier, restore it or remove the claim. If a product shot changes apparent performance, replace it. If the wrong version is exported, stop delivery and reconcile the queue. Preserve prior approved files and increment version numbers.

## 10. Assessment — 100 points
Brief and storyboard 10; master story 15; cutdown adaptation 20; product accuracy and claims 20; audio/graphics 10; technical QC 10; rights/approval 10; handoff 5. Pass 80/100. Rights and claims are mandatory gates.

## 11. Handoff
Submit all exports, project package, claim/rights records, approval notes and usage manifest. State what was actually tested and any unresolved market-specific review.
