# CT-05 Professional Lab 02 — Claim Ledger, Legal-Copy and Version Approval Simulation

- Course: CT-05 — Commercial & Advertisement Editing
- Level: Expert / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 3–4 hours
- Environment: Spreadsheet or issue tracker, any NLE, review player and secure project storage
- Lab status: Authored procedure; not executed, legally reviewed or jurisdiction-certified in this session

## 1. Scenario
A fictional appliance campaign contains three versions (6, 15 and 30 seconds), a product-performance statement, a limited-time offer, a customer testimonial, a music track and AI-assisted product imagery. The learner acts as the editor coordinating evidence, approvals and release readiness.

All products, claims and campaign details must be fictional or learner-created. Do not treat the sample as legal advice or as a substitute for jurisdiction-specific review.

## 2. Learning objectives
- identify express and implied claims in image, text, voice and sequence;
- connect every material claim to evidence and conditions;
- maintain approval status across all versions;
- audit testimonial, music, talent, stock and synthetic-asset permissions;
- create a release gate that prevents unapproved files from being published.

## 3. Required inputs
A fictional brief, product fact sheet, source study or test record, testimonial script/consent record, music license, talent/asset releases, brand guide and three delivery specifications. If an input is unavailable, mark the item blocked; do not invent it.

## 4. Claim ledger schema
| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Claim ID | Unique identifier |
| Exact wording | Spoken, written or implied claim |
| Medium | Voice, super, image, sequence, testimonial |
| Evidence source | Document, test, study or approved source |
| Scope/conditions | Model, date, market, user group and limitations |
| Qualifier | Required context or limitation |
| Versions | 6s / 15s / 30s and exact sequence IDs |
| Reviewer | Authorized factual/legal/brand reviewer |
| Status | Draft / Evidence pending / Review / Approved / Rejected |
| Decision record | Date, version and rationale |

## 5. Procedure
1. Duplicate the project and identify the approved master and all derivative sequences.
2. Watch each edit and list every express and implied claim, including claims created by visual juxtaposition.
3. Assign unique claim IDs and exact wording; avoid paraphrasing away qualifiers.
4. Match each claim to evidence and record conditions, date, product/model and market.
5. Identify claims whose evidence is absent, expired, ambiguous or narrower than the wording.
6. Flag testimonials for authenticity, permission, context and any required disclosure.
7. Verify music, talent, location, stock and graphic licenses for the actual media, territory, term and platform.
8. Audit AI-generated or materially altered assets for product accuracy, provenance, permissions and disclosure obligations.
9. Route claims to the authorized client/legal reviewer; the editor records the decision but does not self-approve regulated claims.
10. Compare 6-, 15- and 30-second versions to ensure each retains necessary context and readable legal copy.
11. Export review copies with visible version IDs and watermarked REVIEW status if appropriate.
12. Record feedback as individual change requests and update affected versions.
13. Re-run the claim and rights audit after every substantive edit.
14. Move a file to approved delivery only when every mandatory gate has recorded approval.
15. Generate a manifest listing exact filenames, checksums if available, specification, claim-ledger revision, license scope and approval record.

## 6. Mandatory release gates
- No unsupported objective claim.
- No material qualifier removed to fit runtime.
- No unlicensed music, stock, font, footage or likeness.
- No unapproved testimonial or synthetic endorsement.
- No generated visual that materially changes product features or performance.
- No ambiguous “final” file; approved version is identified exactly.
- No public release while required legal/brand/client approvals are pending.

## 7. Test cases
| Test | Input condition | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Unsupported performance claim | No source evidence | Block / remove / hold |
| Evidence limited to one model | Edit depicts whole product range | Qualify scope or change visuals |
| 6-second qualifier unreadable | Text too small/brief | Rebuild or remove claim |
| Testimonial release excludes paid ad | Paid campaign placement | Block pending expanded permission |
| Music license excludes social ads | Social campaign | Replace or obtain correct license |
| AI product image adds feature | Image differs from product | Reject/correct and log |
| Old offer date | Offer expired | Block until updated and re-approved |
| Client changes CTA after approval | New CTA in one derivative | Increment version and retest affected outputs |

## 8. Expected deliverables
- completed claim ledger;
- asset-rights register;
- approval matrix and decision log;
- marked-up review copies of all three versions;
- change request log;
- release gate checklist;
- final manifest and QC report;
- short reflection identifying unresolved assumptions.

## 9. Assessment — 100 points
Claim identification 20; evidence/scope matching 20; rights and testimonial audit 15; cross-version qualifier review 15; approval governance 10; version traceability 10; QC and handoff 10. Pass: 80/100. Unsupported claims, unauthorized likeness/music use or false approval status are mandatory remediation failures.

## 10. Completion checklist
- [ ] All claims listed, including implied claims.
- [ ] Evidence and limitations linked.
- [ ] Reviewer and approval state recorded.
- [ ] Testimonial permissions verified.
- [ ] Music/talent/asset rights checked for intended use.
- [ ] AI asset provenance and accuracy reviewed.
- [ ] Each duration checked independently.
- [ ] All outputs identified by exact version.
- [ ] No release gate remains blocked.
- [ ] QC evidence and manifest delivered.

## 11. Professional note
This exercise teaches editorial controls, not legal determinations. Applicable advertising, consumer-protection, privacy, disclosure and intellectual-property rules depend on the jurisdiction, product and media placement. Obtain qualified review before real-world publication.
