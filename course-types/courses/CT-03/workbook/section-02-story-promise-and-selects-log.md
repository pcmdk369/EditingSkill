# CT-03 Workbook Section 02 — Story Promise and Selects Log

- **Course:** CT-03 — Short-Form & Social Video Editing
- **Linked lesson:** [Lesson 02 — Hooks, Story Compression and Retention](../lesson-02-hooks-story-compression-and-retention.md)
- **Purpose:** Convert source footage into a traceable short-form paper edit without losing factual context or essential evidence.
- **Use:** Complete with learner-created or properly licensed media. Mark unknown facts, rights or timecodes as pending; never invent them.

### Promise integrity test

- What exact words or image create the viewer's expectation?
- Which source segment fulfils that expectation?
- What important fact could make the opening misleading?
- Does the footage prove the claim, merely illustrate it, or provide an opinion?
- What narrower promise would be accurate if the source is incomplete?

### Review prompts

1. Where does the viewer need time to inspect detail?
2. Which cut changes chronology or implies a cause?
3. Does any music/effect imply an event not shown?
4. Is a pattern interrupt adding evidence or merely decoration?
5. Does the audio-only sequence remain understandable?
6. Does the silent visual sequence communicate the central idea where required?

## I. Learner reflection

1. Which source moment most strongly supports the story promise?
2. Which cut was hardest to make without losing context?
3. What did reviewers misunderstand, and what edit change addressed it?
4. What can you conclude from your review sample, and what can you not conclude?
5. Which facts, rights or approvals remain pending?

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Story promise is specific and evidence-backed.
- [ ] Three hook options were considered and rejected options have reasons.
- [ ] Selects log includes source identifiers and time ranges/descriptions.
- [ ] Paper edit preserves chronology, causality and necessary qualifications.
- [ ] Compression decisions explain what was removed and why.
- [ ] Pacing is justified by comprehension and information complexity.
- [ ] Two-opening comparison controls and limitations are recorded.
- [ ] Captions, audio and accessibility are reviewed.
- [ ] Rights, privacy, consent and factual integrity checks are documented.
- [ ] Versions and reviewer feedback are traceable.
