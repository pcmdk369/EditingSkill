### Aspect-ratio calculation practice

For a source of 1920 × 1080 pixels:

1. Source aspect ratio = 1920 ÷ 1080 = 1.777… (16:9).
2. A 9:16 crop retaining 1080 pixels of height has width = 1080 × 9 ÷ 16 = 607.5 pixels.
3. A center crop therefore retains only about 608 pixels of the original 1920-pixel width.
4. Record which subject, action or context could be lost.
5. Choose a shot-specific reframe, alternate shot, split composition or new source recording.

Do not treat this arithmetic as a platform specification; it only describes crop geometry.

## E. Accessibility and audience review

- Caption source: learner transcript / human transcript / automated draft with human correction / not applicable (explain)
- Who checks names, numbers, technical terms and speaker labels?
- Is essential information understandable without sound?
- Is speech intelligible without relying on captions alone?
- Are text size, contrast, line breaks and reading time suitable?
- Are faces, demonstrations and important objects unobstructed?
- Is meaning communicated by more than color alone?
- Are rapid flashes, strobing or unnecessarily intense motion avoided?
- Is translation reviewed by a competent human speaker?
- What additional accommodation is required by the brief?

## G. Acceptance tests

| T03 | Caption accuracy | Compare text with source audio | No material transcription error | | |
