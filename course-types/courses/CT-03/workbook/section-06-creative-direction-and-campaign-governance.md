## 11. Accessibility and localization review

- [ ] Captions match speech and meaningful sound information.
- [ ] Text is readable at intended display size.
- [ ] Contrast and hierarchy are adequate; color is not the sole signal.
- [ ] Essential information is not conveyed only by audio or visuals.
- [ ] Essential text avoids destination overlays after actual placement review.
- [ ] Names, dates, units, prices and claims survive localization accurately.
- [ ] Language reviewer and approval are recorded.
- [ ] Exceptions have an owner and rationale.
