# CT-03 — Knowledge Assessment and Answer Key

**Course:** Short-Form & Social Video Editing  
**Coverage:** Audience/platform design, hooks/story compression, vertical reframing, captions and delivery  
**Suggested time:** 35–45 minutes  
**Total:** 40 points

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20 points)

Choose one answer.

1. What should guide sequence dimensions?
   - A. The editor's default preset
   - B. The brief and destination requirements
   - C. The largest number available
   - D. The music tempo

2. Aspect ratio describes:
   - A. Pixel count only
   - B. Frames per second
   - C. Width-to-height proportion
   - D. Audio bit depth

3. A truthful hook:
   - A. Promises a result regardless of evidence
   - B. Creates interest supported by the content
   - C. Removes all context
   - D. Uses the loudest sound

4. Story compression should:
   - A. Remove every pause
   - B. Preserve meaning while removing repetition
   - C. Make all shots equal length
   - D. Add more transitions

5. A selects log should include:
   - A. Only clip colors
   - B. Source filenames and timecodes
   - C. Social account passwords
   - D. Export file size only

6. Why may a landscape clip fail when center-cropped to vertical?
   - A. Vertical has no pixels
   - B. It may remove the subject or relevant context
   - C. Audio is automatically deleted
   - D. Frame rate doubles

7. A keyframe can be used to:
   - A. Change position over time
   - B. Grant copyright permission
   - C. Correct a transcript automatically
   - D. Increase source resolution

8. Burned-in captions are:
   - A. Separate subtitle files
   - B. Text rendered into the video image
   - C. Metadata only
   - D. Audio descriptions

9. Automatic transcription should be:
   - A. Published without review
   - B. Checked against the actual audio
   - C. Used as legal proof
   - D. Replaced with music

10. The final export should be:
    - A. Assumed correct if the timeline looks right
    - B. Played back and checked against the specification
    - C. Renamed without opening it
    - D. Uploaded before review

## Part B — Short answer (4 × 3 = 12 points)

11. Explain the difference between aspect ratio and resolution.
12. Name three elements of a useful short-form deliverable specification.
13. Give two methods to preserve both a speaker and an object when a vertical crop cannot show both.
14. Explain why retention metrics do not prove factual accuracy.

## Part C — Applied reasoning (8 points)

15. A 45-second product video contains a 5-second opening, 20 seconds of repeated product rotations, 10 seconds of a useful feature demonstration and 10 seconds of CTA. The target is 25 seconds. Propose a revised structure and identify what must not be removed. (4 points)

16. A caption says “fifteen” but the speaker says “fifty.” Describe the correction and the validation step. (4 points)

11. Aspect ratio is the proportional relationship of width and height; resolution is the number of pixels in each dimension. (3)
12. Any three: dimensions/aspect ratio, frame rate, duration, codec, captions, audio, platform, CTA, file constraints. (1 each)
13. Any two: cut between close-ups, use keyframed reframing, split composition, use a wider vertical-safe source, or redesign the shot order. (1.5 each)
14. Retention indicates viewing behavior, not whether claims are true, complete or useful. (3)
15. Example: 3-second truthful hook, 4-second context, 10-second feature evidence, 4-second result and 4-second CTA. Preserve the feature demonstration and any context needed to interpret the claim. Other defensible structures earn full credit. (4)
16. Correct the caption to match the recording, replay the relevant audio/video at normal speed, inspect surrounding context and update transcript/sidecar/burned-in versions consistently. (4)

## Performance bands

- 36–40: Strong command; ready for independent practical work.
- 32–35: Competent; minor revision needed.
- 28–31: Developing; targeted practice recommended.
- Below 28: Revisit the lessons and retake after remediation.

## Remediation

- Missed 1–2: repeat Lesson 01 and revise the specification sheet.
- Missed 3–5: repeat Lesson 02 and rebuild the selects/story map.
- Missed 6–10: repeat Lesson 03 and validate a vertical export.
- Missed applied questions: complete Professional Lab 01 with reviewer feedback.
