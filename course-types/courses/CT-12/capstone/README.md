# CT-12 Capstone — Matched Scene and Approved Creative Look

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–16 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Grade a 2–3 minute scene or a 10-shot sequence from authorized footage. Normalize camera sources, match shots, create a documented look and deliver an approved master with reference evidence.

## Deliverables
- source/media and color-space inventory;
- monitoring and pipeline diagram;
- primary correction and shot-matching project;
- creative look bible and reference stills;
- node graph/LUT register;
- final graded master;
- metadata/export specification;
- QC report and archive manifest;
- reflection explaining technical and creative decisions.

## Mandatory gates
No unverified HDR/SDR claims, incorrect transforms or unlicensed LUT/assets. Preserve original media and project.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Pipeline and source management | 15 |
| Primary correction | 15 |
| Shot matching | 20 |
| Creative look and consistency | 15 |
| Scopes/reference evidence | 10 |
| Technical export QC | 10 |
| Project/archive handoff | 10 |
| Reflection | 5 |

Pass 80/100. Correct output transform and full playback are mandatory gates.

## Review
Reviewer inspects source transforms, node/LUT order, reference stills, export metadata and full playback. Actual project files and review evidence are required for validation.
