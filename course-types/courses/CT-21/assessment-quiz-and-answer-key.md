# CT-21 — Green Screen & Chroma Key Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. Chroma key separates by: A. Color range B. Audio frequency C. Frame number D. File name.
2. Even screen lighting helps: A. Create a cleaner key B. Change frame rate C. Remove license D. Add resolution.
3. A garbage matte removes: A. Irrelevant regions B. Subject details C. Audio D. Color profile.
4. A holdout matte preserves: A. Details keyer may remove B. Background only C. File metadata D. Audio.
5. Spill is: A. Screen color reflected on subject B. Export size C. Motion blur D. Camera shake.
6. Light wrap: A. Integrates subject edge with background B. Deletes matte C. Changes camera lens D. Creates consent.
7. A floating subject may lack: A. Contact/cast shadow B. Captions C. Audio track D. Filename.
8. Keying presets should be: A. Reviewed per shot B. Applied blindly C. Used without source D. Never changed.
9. AI segmentation should be: A. Reviewed across motion B. Assumed correct C. Used without consent D. Published directly.
10. Delivery should include: A. Plate, matte/project and metadata as required B. Only screenshot C. No source D. Random alpha.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Why should subject and screen be lit separately?
12. Name three causes of poor keying.
13. Why are hair and transparent objects difficult?
14. What makes a subject look poorly integrated into a background?
15. What should be recorded for a green-screen shoot?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. A presenter has green spill, holes in clothing matte and a shadowed screen. Explain capture/post correction and validation. (8)
17. AI segmentation removes a transparent glass object. What is the appropriate correction? (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. It allows uniform screen and independent subject exposure/lighting. (3)
12. Uneven light, shadows, spill, poor separation, motion blur/compression. (3)
13. They contain partial transparency and fine detail. (3)
14. Wrong scale, light, perspective, color, shadow or depth of field. (3)
15. Camera/lens, frame rate, shutter, exposure, white balance, lighting and source metadata. (3)
16. Improve screen lighting, increase separation, refine key/holdout, suppress spill, check subject color and full-motion matte. (8)
17. Create a separate manual matte or preserve glass via alternate pass; inspect across motion and document correction. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Rights and full-motion matte integrity are mandatory.
