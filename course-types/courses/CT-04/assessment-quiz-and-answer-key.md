# CT-04 — Documentary & Interview Editing Assessment

**Coverage:** Research ethics, transcript logging, selects, interview structure, B-roll, fact-checking, AI and professional delivery  
**Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes  
**Total:** 50 points

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. A participant's statement is automatically proof of every external fact it contains.  
   A. True  B. False
2. A transcript should identify:  
   A. Source and timecode  B. Only topic  C. Export bitrate  D. Music tempo
3. A select should preserve:  
   A. Only the dramatic sentence  B. Enough context to confirm meaning  C. No source link  D. Only reaction shots
4. A J-cut means:  
   A. Audio begins before its corresponding picture  B. Picture begins first  C. A title animation  D. A color transition
5. B-roll may be misleading when:  
   A. It is relevant  B. It is clearly labeled  C. It implies an unverified place or date  D. It is logged
6. A claim ledger records:  
   A. Factual statements and supporting sources  B. Only music cues  C. Camera settings  D. Social likes
7. If a quote is uncertain, the editor should:  
   A. Invent a likely word  B. Flag and verify it  C. Delete the source  D. Ask AI to decide
8. AI-generated transcription should be:  
   A. Published without review  B. Compared with source audio  C. Treated as legal proof  D. Used to replace consent
9. Delivery permission automatically grants portfolio publication permission.  
   A. True  B. False
10. Picture lock should include:  
    A. Traceable approval and controlled change process  B. Untracked edits  C. No QC  D. No backup

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Explain the difference between testimony and independently verified fact.
12. Name three fields required in a selects log.
13. Give two examples of misleading B-roll use.
14. What should happen when a material factual claim cannot be verified?
15. Why is participant consent scope important when archiving and publishing?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. An interviewee says: “The factory closed in 2022, and everyone lost their jobs.” An AI transcript changes 2022 to 2023. A present-day exterior shot is available, but its location is unconfirmed. Describe a responsible edit and verification plan. (8)
17. A client approves the film for a private screening but not public release. What can be included in a portfolio? Explain. (7)

## Answer key
1. B
2. A
3. B
4. A
5. C
6. A
7. B
8. B
9. B
10. A

11. Testimony documents what a person reports; external claims require corroboration appropriate to their significance. (3)
12. Any three: source filename, speaker, in/out timecode, theme, context note, rights status. (3)
13. Present-day footage presented as archival; wrong location used to imply a specific event; cutaway implying an unverified reaction. (3)
14. Verify, attribute as allegation/opinion, qualify or omit; record unresolved status. (3)
15. Permission is limited by scope, purpose and restrictions; delivery, archive and public display may be distinct uses. (3)
16. Re-listen to source and correct transcript only after confirmation; ask participant/producer to clarify date; verify closure and employment claims independently; do not state “everyone” without evidence; do not identify unconfirmed exterior; label current footage if location/date verified; record uncertainty and approval. (8)
17. Do not publicly display the film or identifying excerpts without separate authorization. A portfolio may describe permitted, non-confidential process at a high level if contract/privacy allows, without revealing protected content. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 excellent; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 requires remediation. Any rights/consent breach or fabricated quote requires remediation irrespective of score.
