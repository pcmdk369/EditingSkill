# CT-04 Capstone — Three-Minute Interview-Led Mini-Documentary

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–16 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and independent review not evidenced

## Brief
Create a three-minute documentary using at least one authorized interview and supporting visual material. Establish a clear question, a coherent sequence and a conclusion proportionate to the evidence. The work may leave a question unresolved when the sources do not justify a definitive answer.

## Required deliverables
- 3-minute master export (duration tolerance defined by the brief);
- editable project and linked media package;
- research brief and source register;
- timecoded transcript and selects log;
- paper edit / sequence map;
- factual claim ledger with source references;
- B-roll/archival provenance log;
- participant releases and rights log (stored securely);
- captions/subtitles and transcript;
- QC report, version history and final approval record;
- 500–800 word editorial reflection.

## Mandatory ethical criteria
No fabricated quotations, misleading chronology, unverified attribution or unauthorized footage. Distinguish testimony, allegation, opinion, inference and corroborated fact. Protect sensitive participant information. Use external AI processing only with appropriate authorization and privacy review.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Research and story question | 10 |
| Interview structure and quote integrity | 15 |
| Documentary story clarity and pacing | 15 |
| B-roll accuracy and visual continuity | 10 |
| Fact-checking and attribution | 15 |
| Rights, consent and participant care | 15 |
| Audio, captions and technical delivery | 10 |
| Project organization and reflection | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Pass: 80/100. Fabricated quote, material misrepresentation, unauthorized use or serious consent breach is a critical failure requiring correction regardless of numeric score.

## Review protocol
Reviewer traces a sample of every important quote to source timecodes, audits all factual claims and rights records, watches the complete export and records defects. Course validation requires actual learner artifacts and documented review; this brief alone does not validate a project.
