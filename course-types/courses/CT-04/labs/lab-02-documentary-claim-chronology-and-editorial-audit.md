# CT-04 Professional Lab 02 — Documentary Claim Ledger, Chronology and Editorial Audit

- Course: CT-04 — Documentary & Interview Editing
- Level: Expert / Professional
- Estimated time: 4–6 hours
- Environment: NLE, transcript/logging tool, secure source register and claim ledger
- Lab status: Authored procedure; no external factual/legal review is performed in this session

## 1. Brief
Build a 2–3 minute documentary sequence from authorized interview, archival and present-day material. Audit chronology, attribution, B-roll context and every factual claim before picture lock.

## 2. Learning objectives
- trace every important statement to source and timecode;
- distinguish testimony, allegation, opinion, inference and corroborated fact;
- maintain a chronology and claim ledger;
- avoid misleading archival/present-day juxtaposition;
- conduct a documented editorial review and controlled revision.

## 3. Inputs and rights
Use learner-created or properly licensed interviews, archival materials, stills, documents and footage. Record participant consent and permitted uses. Keep sensitive records access-controlled. Do not send confidential material to an external transcription/research service without authorization.

## 4. Claim ledger
| Field | Required detail |
|---|---|
| Claim ID | Unique reference |
| Exact wording | Spoken/narrated/on-screen statement |
| Type | Testimony / allegation / opinion / inference / verified fact |
| Source | Interview/document/archive reference |
| Timecode/page | Exact location |
| Corroboration | Independent evidence and limitations |
| Attribution | Who said it / source credit |
| Status | Draft / Verify / Qualified / Approved / Removed |
| Reviewer | Responsible factual/editorial approver |

## 5. Procedure
1. Freeze and inventory all source files; record origin/date/owner/rights.
2. Review full interviews and create timecoded transcripts.
3. Create a chronology with known dates, uncertain dates and conflicting accounts.
4. Build a paper edit with quote IDs and source timecodes.
5. Extract every factual claim from dialogue, narration, titles and juxtaposed visuals.
6. Classify claim type and connect to evidence.
7. Verify material claims against reliable independent records where appropriate.
8. Preserve uncertainty and conflicting evidence; do not manufacture resolution.
9. Audit B-roll and archival material for date, location and context.
10. Check that cutaways do not imply a reaction, event or place not established by source.
11. Conduct a viewer-comprehension review and a separate factual/rights review.
12. Record every change request and reason.
13. Export a review cut and trace a sample of quotes/claims back to source.
14. Lock only after required approvals; archive decision log and source manifest.

## 6. Validation matrix
| Test | Method | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Quote traceability | Select sample and inspect full source | Correct speaker/context/timecode |
| Chronology | Compare timeline to source records | Uncertain dates marked |
| Claim integrity | Audit ledger | No unsupported fact presented as established |
| B-roll context | Inspect every archival/cutaway shot | No misleading date/place/reaction |
| Rights/consent | Review release/license | Use within scope |
| Review | Inspect feedback and changes | Decisions documented |
| Export | Watch complete sequence | No missing media/sync defects |

## 7. Troubleshooting
- Conflicting dates: show uncertainty and seek corroboration.
- Quote lacks context: restore surrounding material or remove.
- Archival source unclear: label cautiously or omit.
- Claim cannot be verified: attribute, qualify, remove or hold.
- Participant disputes representation: return to source, document review and follow agreed process.

## 8. Deliverables
Source inventory, chronology, transcript/selects, paper edit, claim ledger, B-roll provenance, review cut, decision log, rights register and QC report.

## 9. Assessment — 100 points
Traceability 20; chronology 15; claim classification/verification 20; B-roll integrity 15; rights/consent 10; review governance 10; documentation/QC 10. Pass 80/100. Fabricated quotes or material misrepresentation are critical failures.

## 10. Completion checklist
- [ ] Sources and rights documented.
- [ ] Quotes trace to full source.
- [ ] Chronology records uncertainty.
- [ ] Claims classified and evidenced.
- [ ] Archival/B-roll context checked.
- [ ] Separate factual and editorial reviews recorded.
- [ ] Revision and picture-lock history archived.
