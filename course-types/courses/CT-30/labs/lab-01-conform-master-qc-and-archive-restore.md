# CT-30 Professional Lab 01 — Conform, Master QC and Archive Restore

- Level: Expert / Professional
- Estimated time: 6–8 hours
- Environment: NLE, media inspector, checksum utility and secure archive storage
- Lab status: Authored procedure; no specific NLE interchange or archive platform is certified

## Brief
Conform an approved short-form sequence to full-quality sources, produce one master and two derivatives, run independent QC and prove that the archive can be restored.

## Procedure
1. Record approved sequence ID, frame rate, duration, timecode and delivery specification.
2. Inventory proxy and original media; map source IDs and checksums where available.
3. Export a supported interchange file or use a verified project relink workflow.
4. Relink to original sources and inspect all media online.
5. Compare offline/online cuts, transitions, effects, titles and audio sync.
6. Resolve missing assets and document unsupported effects/plugins.
7. Collect color, audio, captions and graphics sign-offs.
8. Render a short technical proof and compare to approved reference.
9. Render master and two derivatives to separate versioned output folders.
10. Inspect metadata and watch every export completely.
11. Log defects with severity/timecode; correct on a new version and retest.
12. Create manifest and checksums for approved outputs and archive assets.
13. Copy archive to agreed redundant storage.
14. Restore to a clean folder/location and reopen project; verify files/checksums.
15. Record limitations, receipt and archive retention schedule.

## Validation
Conform matches approved edit; all dependencies resolve; master and derivatives match their own specifications; QC defects closed or accepted by authorized owner; checksum/restore test passes.

## Deliverables
Conformed project, master, derivatives, sign-off records, QC report, manifest/checksums and restore report.

## Assessment — 100 points
Conform accuracy 20; specialist handoff 10; master/derivative delivery 15; QC evidence 20; version control 10; archive/checksum 15; documentation 10. Pass 80/100.
