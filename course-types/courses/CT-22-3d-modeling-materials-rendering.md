# 3D Modeling, Materials & Rendering

**Course ID:** CT-22  
**Pathway:** Foundation → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Expert → Master → AI & Automation → Professional Practice

## Purpose and outcomes
Develop 3D asset skills from topology and scene setup through polygon modeling, UVs, materials, lighting, procedural systems, art direction, responsible AI assistance and production delivery.

Learners will model clean geometry, unwrap UVs, create materials, light/render assets, optimize scenes, validate AI-generated meshes and deliver reproducible native/interchange packages.

## Software track
Use a current 3D application with mesh, UV, material and rendering features. Record units, axis, renderer, app/version and hardware. Test interchange exports in the target application.

## Completion requirements
Pass the 50-point assessment, submit lab and capstone, meet rights/topology/interchange gates and provide reproducible source package. Suggested capstone pass: 80/100. Independent review and learner execution evidence remain required.
