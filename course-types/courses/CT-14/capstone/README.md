# CT-14 Capstone — Professional Interview Podcast Episode

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–16 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Produce a 10–15 minute interview podcast episode with two speakers, a consistent series identity and a documented delivery package.

## Deliverables
- recording and consent plan;
- original host/guest files and metadata;
- editable multitrack session;
- final stereo master;
- intro/outro and licensed music record;
- transcript and captions where required;
- two promotional clips only if authorized;
- loudness/true-peak and sync reports;
- guest/music rights register;
- session dependency manifest and archive note;
- editorial policy and reflection.

## Mandatory gates
Preserve source audio, maintain guest meaning, obtain consent for recording and clips, do not synthesize voices without explicit authorization, and verify final export against actual publisher specs.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Recording/source quality | 10 |
| Dialogue edit and pacing | 15 |
| Voice cleanup and mix | 15 |
| Remote sync and routing | 15 |
| Music/series identity | 10 |
| Transcript/accessibility | 10 |
| Rights, consent and privacy | 10 |
| Technical delivery and handoff | 15 |

Pass: 80/100. Consent, factual attribution and technical delivery are mandatory gates.

## Review
Reviewer listens to full episode and clips, checks transcript, rights, sync, loudness and session packaging. Actual learner outputs and review evidence are required for validation.
