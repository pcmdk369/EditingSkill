# CT-14 — Dialogue, Voice & Podcast Editing Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. Room tone is: A. Background sound without foreground speech B. Music bed C. Silence file D. Noise gate.
2. A plosive is: A. Air burst causing a pop B. High sample rate C. Stereo pan D. Caption cue.
3. A de-esser reduces: A. Excessive sibilance B. Room echo always C. File size D. Sample rate.
4. Compression controls: A. Dynamic range B. Copyright C. Sync offset D. Track naming.
5. Remote recordings should be checked for drift: A. At multiple points B. Only start C. Only end D. Never.
6. A transcript should be: A. Verified against audio B. Published without review C. Guessed from context D. Replaced by metadata.
7. A guest release defines: A. Usage permissions B. Sample rate C. Export codec D. Loudness.
8. AI voice cloning requires: A. Explicit authorization B. Only a model prompt C. No consent D. Automatic approval.
9. Podcast loudness target should come from: A. Actual publisher/client specification B. Guess C. One universal number D. Track count.
10. Promotional clips should be checked for: A. Context and rights B. Only thumbnail C. Only file size D. Nothing.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Why is room tone useful?
12. Name three dialogue processing tools and their roles.
13. What causes remote sync drift?
14. Name three transcript items that need careful human review.
15. What should a professional podcast handoff contain?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. A 45-minute remote interview drifts by the final segment and AI transcription misattributes a statement. Explain diagnosis and correction. (8)
17. A guest agrees to an episode but refuses social clips. What can the producer publish? (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. It bridges edits and preserves acoustic continuity. (3)
12. EQ, compression, denoise, de-essing, fades—roles as defined. (3)
13. Independent clocks, sample-rate mismatch, variable frame rate or recording drift. (3)
14. Names, numbers, negation, speaker attribution, timestamps. (3)
15. Master, clips if authorized, transcript, session/dependencies, rights log, QC and manifest. (3)
16. Inspect source sample rates/clocks and sync points; align on copy, verify throughout; compare transcript with original tracks/video and correct attribution; record correction and retest export. (8)
17. Publish only the approved episode within release scope; do not publish clips until separate permission is obtained. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Consent/rights and misattribution require correction.
