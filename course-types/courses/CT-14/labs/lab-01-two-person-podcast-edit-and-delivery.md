# CT-14 Professional Lab 01 — Two-Person Podcast Edit and Delivery

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 4–6 hours
- Environment: DAW/audio editor; record OS/app/version and interface
- Lab status: Authored procedure; not executed in this session

## Brief
Edit a five-minute two-person remote conversation. Deliver an intelligible stereo mix, two promotional clips, reviewed transcript and editable session.

## Prerequisites and rights
Complete CT-14 Lessons 01–05. Use learner-recorded or licensed speech with guest consent. Confirm music rights and publisher delivery targets.

## Procedure
1. Inventory host/guest tracks and source metadata.
2. Preserve originals and create a labeled multitrack session.
3. Align tracks with a clap/waveform event.
4. Check sync at start, middle and end.
5. Edit false starts and repetition while preserving meaning and natural pauses.
6. Use room tone to bridge edits.
7. Apply conservative cleanup, EQ and dynamics to each voice.
8. Balance host and guest without erasing their tonal differences.
9. Add licensed intro/outro music and automate beneath speech.
10. Create two clips and verify each is authorized for promotional use.
11. Create and review transcript against audio.
12. Measure final rendered mix against supplied loudness/peak specification.
13. Export and play all deliverables.
14. Package session, rights log, manifest and correction record.

## Expected artifacts
Five-minute mix, two clips, transcript, session, sync report, loudness report, guest/music rights log and QC manifest.

## Validation
| Test | Method | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Sync | Multiple timeline points | No drift |
| Dialogue | Full playback | Both speakers intelligible |
| Edit quality | Listen at normal speed | Natural pauses, no clicks |
| Music | Rights and mix check | Licensed, speech clear |
| Transcript | Compare with audio | Accurate names/negation/speakers |
| Loudness | Measure rendered file | Meets supplied target |
| Clips | Check consent and context | No unauthorized/misleading excerpt |

## Troubleshooting
Remote drift: inspect source clock/sample rate. Voice mismatch: adjust gain/EQ individually. Transcript error: correct against audio. Clip not cleared: withhold until guest/rights approval.

## Assessment — 100 points
Recording/source handling 10; dialogue edit 15; voice processing 15; sync 15; mix/music 10; transcript/accessibility 10; rights/consent 10; export/handoff 15. Pass 80/100; rights and meaning preservation are mandatory.

## Handoff
Submit all exports, transcript, session, sync/loudness reports, rights log, manifest and correction notes.
