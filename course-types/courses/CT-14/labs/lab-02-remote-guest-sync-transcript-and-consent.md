# CT-14 Professional Lab 02 — Remote Guest Sync, Transcript Accuracy and Consent Handoff

- Course: CT-14 — Dialogue, Voice & Podcast Editing
- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 4–5 hours
- Environment: DAW/audio editor, remote recording files, transcript tool (optional), secure storage
- Lab status: Authored procedure; no external recording service or transcription model is certified here

## 1. Scenario
Produce a 20-minute two-person interview from locally recorded tracks. Each speaker has a separate recording with different start times and room tone. Deliver a clean episode, aligned speaker tracks, reviewed transcript and a consent-aware archive package.

## 2. Learning objectives
- synchronize independent recordings and detect drift;
- preserve speaker meaning while editing;
- review automated transcripts against source;
- protect guest privacy, consent and withdrawal/retention requirements;
- deliver master, stems, transcript and session dependencies.

## 3. Inputs and authorization
Use learner-created recordings or interviews with permission for editing and intended distribution. Confirm whether recording, editing, publication, transcript, clips, promotional use and portfolio use are separately covered. Do not upload confidential speech to external services without approval and privacy review.

## 4. Procedure
1. Create a secure project folder and verify backup.
2. Inventory each speaker file; record duration, sample rate, channels and source time.
3. Identify sync markers or shared spoken events and align tracks.
4. Check sync at beginning, middle and end; investigate drift before editing.
5. Preserve a raw copy of every speaker recording.
6. Build a transcript draft with speaker IDs and timecodes.
7. Human-check names, numbers, negations, technical terms and overlapping speech.
8. Edit for clarity while retaining context, intent and meaningful pauses.
9. Use room tone and crossfades to avoid abrupt edits; do not remove breaths mechanically.
10. Apply conservative voice processing and compare against unprocessed reference.
11. Create a transcript with uncertainty flags and a correction log.
12. Confirm guest-approved name, pronouns or attribution only from their supplied instructions; do not infer.
13. Export full episode, isolated speaker tracks/stems and transcript in requested formats.
14. Verify loudness and technical specifications against the actual brief.
15. Create a guest-facing handoff record and archive according to the agreed retention policy.

## 5. Consent and privacy checklist
- Recording permission and intended audience.
- Editing and excerpt/clip permission.
- Transcript and translation use.
- Promotional/social use.
- External AI/transcription processing permission.
- Name/attribution preferences.
- Sensitive topic handling and approved corrections.
- Retention, access and deletion schedule.
- Portfolio permission separately recorded.

## 6. Validation matrix
| Test | Method | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Sync | Start/middle/end comparison | No unexplained drift |
| Transcript | Full listen and text comparison | Names/numbers/negation accurate |
| Context | Compare edits with full answer | No meaning reversal |
| Voice quality | Listen at normal playback | Natural and intelligible |
| Loudness | Measure full program | Matches brief |
| Guest consent | Review authorization record | Intended uses covered |
| Files | Reopen all exports | Complete and readable |
| Archive | Check access and retention | Follows agreed policy |

## 7. Troubleshooting
- Drift grows over time: inspect source frame/sample clock and resampling workflow.
- Transcript changes meaning: correct from audio and review adjacent cues.
- Edit sounds unnatural: restore room tone or part of the original pause.
- Guest requests correction: follow agreed editorial policy and document the decision.
- Permission excludes clips: do not publish promotional excerpts until separately authorized.

## 8. Deliverables
Episode master, raw source inventory, aligned project, speaker stems, transcript, correction log, consent/use matrix, QC report and archive manifest.

## 9. Assessment — 100 points
Sync 15; transcript accuracy 20; editorial integrity 15; voice quality 10; consent/privacy 20; technical delivery 10; documentation 10. Pass 80/100. Unauthorized recording/publication or material misquotation is a critical failure.

## 10. Completion checklist
- [ ] Source recordings backed up.
- [ ] Sync checked at multiple points.
- [ ] Transcript human-reviewed.
- [ ] Edits preserve speaker intent.
- [ ] Guest permissions cover actual uses.
- [ ] AI processing authorization checked.
- [ ] Master/stems/transcript reopened and checked.
- [ ] Archive/retention manifest complete.
