# CT-08 — Digital Illustration & Graphic Editing Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. Vector artwork is primarily represented by: A. Paths/shapes B. Pixels only C. Audio samples D. Frames.
2. Raster artwork is made of: A. Nodes only B. Pixels C. Fonts D. Layers only.
3. A Bézier curve is controlled by: A. Anchor points/handles B. Audio gain C. Export bitrate D. Camera focus.
4. Bleed is: A. Extra artwork beyond trim B. A font style C. A color profile D. A watermark.
5. Typography hierarchy guides: A. Attention B. File compression C. Copyright D. Audio sync.
6. Color alone should not carry essential meaning because: A. Accessibility concerns B. File size C. Export speed D. Resolution.
7. Preflight checks: A. Production readiness B. Social engagement C. Music tempo D. Camera metadata.
8. Font license may restrict: A. Embedding/redistribution B. Pixel dimensions C. Layer order D. Aspect ratio.
9. AI-generated logo output is automatically trademark-safe. A. True B. False.
10. A professional handoff includes: A. Editable source and license/asset records B. Only screenshot C. Only a JPEG D. No manifest.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Give two situations where vector is preferable to raster.
12. Explain artboard, safe margin and bleed.
13. Name three factors that influence typography legibility.
14. Why should a logo be tested at small sizes and on different backgrounds?
15. What is a preflight checklist used for?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. A print poster has missing fonts, no bleed and linked images with low resolution. Explain a safe preflight and correction workflow. (8)
17. An AI-generated logo resembles a well-known brand and contains misspelled text. Describe the response and final rebuild process. (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 B; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 B; 10 A.
11. Logos/icons and scalable illustrations. (3)
12. Artboard is the canvas; safe margin protects important content; bleed extends artwork beyond trim. (3)
13. Size, weight, contrast, line length, spacing, typeface and background. (3)
14. It ensures legibility and adaptability across contexts. (3)
15. To identify technical/production problems before delivery. (3)
16. Obtain printer spec, license/replace fonts, embed/outline only as permitted, add bleed, replace low-resolution links, proof PDF and inspect final file. (8)
17. Reject the derivative, do not rely on a prompt to establish originality, research similarity/rights, create a distinct concept and manually build clean editable vectors with licensed type. (7)
