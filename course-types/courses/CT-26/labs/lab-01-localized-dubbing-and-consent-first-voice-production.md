# CT-26 Professional Lab 01 — Localized Dubbing and Consent-First Voice Production

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 5–7 hours
- Environment: DAW/audio editor, authorized speaker/voice, subtitle/script editor and secure storage
- Lab status: Authored; no external voice provider is endorsed or executed

## Brief
Produce a 30–60 second localized dialogue using a consenting speaker or properly licensed synthetic voice that does not imitate an unauthorized real person. Deliver target-language script, voice track, sync notes, approvals and disclosure record.

## Procedure
1. Confirm source recording and rights to translate/adapt.
2. Confirm target language/locale and appoint a competent reviewer.
3. Lock source transcript and target script.
4. Build pronunciation and timing guide.
5. Verify speaker/voice license and permitted use; if synthetic, document provider/model/version and scope.
6. Record or generate a short test passage.
7. Review names, pronunciation, emotion, pace and intelligibility.
8. Align phrases to picture and preserve natural pauses.
9. Mix voice against original ambience/music without masking.
10. Review the entire sequence with a fluent reviewer.
11. Confirm required synthetic-media disclosure and client approval.
12. Export voice stem, full mix, transcript and target script.
13. Reopen all files and validate format, sync, channel layout and loudness target from brief.
14. Package consent, rights, provenance and retention records.

## Validation
| Test | Method | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Script | Compare source/target | Meaning preserved |
| Language | Fluent human review | Names/idioms correct |
| Voice rights | Inspect license/consent | Specific use covered |
| Timing | Playback with picture | Sync and natural phrasing |
| Mix | Listen at normal level | Intelligible and clean |
| Disclosure | Review delivery context | Required label included |
| Privacy | Audit provider/storage | Approved handling |
| Handoff | Reopen package | Files and records complete |

## Deliverables
Source/target scripts, glossary, voice permission/license, provenance log, voice stem, final mix, sync report, human review and manifest.

## Assessment — 100 points
Translation 20; voice performance 15; sync 15; mix 10; consent/rights 20; disclosure/provenance 10; delivery 10. Pass 80/100. Unauthorized voice cloning or deceptive impersonation is a critical failure.
