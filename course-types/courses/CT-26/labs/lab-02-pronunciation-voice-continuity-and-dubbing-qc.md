# CT-26 Professional Lab 02 — Pronunciation, Voice Continuity and Dubbing QC

- Level: Expert / Professional
- Estimated time: 3–5 hours
- Environment: DAW/audio editor, target script, pronunciation guide and video player
- Lab status: Authored; no specific voice vendor or language service is tested

## Brief
Review a localized voice performance for pronunciation, consistency, timing, intelligibility and rights compliance across three short scenes.

## Procedure
1. Confirm source script, target script, locale and approved glossary.
2. Verify voice performer/license scope for scenes, clips and promotional use.
3. Build a pronunciation sheet for names, brands, numbers and technical terms.
4. Record a reference take and document mic/room/gain conditions.
5. Compare all scenes against the reference for tone, distance, noise and pacing.
6. Check phrase timing and visible lip-sync; flag lines that require script adaptation.
7. Have a fluent reviewer check pronunciation, idiom, tone and meaning.
8. Compare target performance to source intent without requiring an identical emotional delivery.
9. Inspect edits, room tone, crossfades, breaths and processing artifacts.
10. Validate synthetic voice provenance/disclosure if applicable.
11. Export review mix and isolated voice stem; check complete playback.
12. Record defects, corrections, approvals and final version.

## Validation
Script fidelity, pronunciation, voice continuity, sync, mix quality, rights/consent, disclosure and handoff must pass independently.

## Deliverables
Pronunciation guide, continuity notes, reviewed target script, voice stem, final mix, language-review sign-off and QC report.

## Assessment — 100 points
Language accuracy 20; performance continuity 15; sync 15; audio quality 15; rights/consent 20; documentation 15. Pass 80/100. Unauthorized voice imitation is a critical failure.
