# CT-25 Capstone — Human-Reviewed AI-Assisted Video Edit

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–16 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and independent review not evidenced

## Brief
Create a short edit using at least one AI-assisted task such as transcription, scene detection, highlight suggestions, reframing or enhancement. Preserve source truth and document all AI contributions and human decisions.

## Required deliverables
- brief and source inventory;
- rights/consent/privacy assessment;
- provider/model/version and settings log;
- AI-generated transcript/selects/labels;
- human correction and rejection log;
- source-linked paper edit;
- final video and captions as specified;
- technical QC report;
- provenance and disclosure notes;
- editable project and dependency manifest;
- reflection describing limitations and human value.

## Mandatory gates
No unauthorized upload, synthetic impersonation, fabricated evidence or unreviewed AI output. Preserve originals and distinguish generated drafts from approved content.

## Rubric — 100 points
Brief/task selection 10; rights/privacy 15; AI output evaluation 15; editorial quality/context 20; human review 15; technical QC 10; provenance/handoff 15. Pass 80/100.

## Review protocol
Reviewer traces selections to source, checks provider/rights records, inspects output continuity, watches full export and confirms the final decision log. This brief alone does not validate execution.
