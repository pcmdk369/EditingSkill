# CT-25 Professional Lab 01 — AI-Assisted Short Video and Human QC

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 5–7 hours
- Environment: Approved AI video tool and conventional NLE; record provider/model/version
- Lab status: Authored procedure; not executed in this session

## Brief
Create a 30–45 second short video from learner-created footage using at least one bounded AI task such as transcription, scene logging or smart reframing. Human editor owns story, accuracy and final export.

## Prerequisites
Complete CT-25 Lessons 01–05. Use non-sensitive, authorized footage. Confirm provider terms and data handling.

## Procedure
1. Define audience, message and output specification.
2. Inventory source footage and preserve originals.
3. Assess privacy/rights and choose a suitable AI task.
4. Review provider terms, retention and commercial use.
5. Generate transcript/scene/reframe draft on copies.
6. Correct names, timestamps and scene labels against source.
7. Review full context of suggested highlights.
8. Assemble human-approved edit in NLE.
9. Check crop, subject/object visibility and continuity.
10. Identify and document any generated video segments.
11. Export proof and inspect metadata/full playback.
12. Complete provenance, disclosure and QC manifest.

## Expected artifacts
Original media, AI draft, human-reviewed project, final export, transcript/scene log, provider record, rights/privacy checklist and QC report.

## Validation
| Test | Method | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Source integrity | Compare originals | Unchanged |
| Transcript | Audio comparison | Accurate words/speakers |
| Highlights | Review full context | No misleading omission |
| Reframing | Full-motion inspection | Important subjects/objects visible |
| Generated content | Frame review | No unapproved identity/factual changes |
| Export | Metadata/full playback | Meets brief |
| Provenance | Manifest | Source/model/version/reviewer recorded |

## Troubleshooting
ASR error: correct against audio. Crop loses product: reframe manually. Model update: stop batch and pin/version. Generated factual footage: reject or label as illustrative with approval.

## Assessment — 100 points
Task selection 10; source/rights assessment 15; AI output evaluation 15; editorial accuracy 20; reframing/continuity 10; provenance/disclosure 10; export QC 10; handoff 10. Pass 80/100; rights/privacy/factual gates are mandatory.

## Handoff
Submit original, AI draft, reviewed project, final export, provenance, rights log and QC manifest.
