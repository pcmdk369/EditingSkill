# CT-25 — AI-Assisted Video Editing Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. ASR means: A. Automatic speech recognition B. Audio sample rate C. Aspect scale ratio D. Automated screen render.
2. AI highlights should be: A. Reviewed in full context B. Published automatically C. Treated as fact D. Used without source.
3. Smart reframing should preserve: A. Important subjects/objects B. Only faces C. Only center D. Nothing else.
4. Model drift means: A. Output changes due to model/version B. Camera movement C. Audio sync D. File compression.
5. Generated video can: A. Invent events/details B. Prove real events C. Guarantee continuity D. Grant consent.
6. Batch workflows need: A. Provenance and exception handling B. No logs C. Overwrite originals D. No review.
7. Voice/face synthesis requires: A. Explicit authorization B. Only a prompt C. No disclosure D. No source rights.
8. Provider terms should include review of: A. Data handling and commercial use B. Only UI color C. Only render time D. None.
9. Human approval is necessary for: A. Editorial truth and final use B. Nothing C. Only filename D. Only thumbnail.
10. Final export should be: A. Played and checked B. Assumed correct C. Only timeline preview D. Uploaded without review.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Why can AI scene labels be inaccurate?
12. What should be checked in a smart crop?
13. Why is generated video not factual evidence?
14. Name four fields in an AI video provenance manifest.
15. What is the purpose of human approval gates?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. AI highlight extraction removes a qualifier and changes the meaning of a speaker's statement. Explain the correction. (8)
17. A provider updates its model halfway through a 100-clip batch. Explain how to maintain reproducibility. (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. Models infer patterns and can miss context, nuance or scene boundaries. (3)
12. Subject/object visibility, movement, text, continuity and safe area. (3)
13. It may depict synthetic events that never occurred. (3)
14. Source ID, model/version, settings, output, reviewer, exceptions, approval. (3)
15. To ensure a responsible human approves meaning, rights, safety and publication. (3)
16. Restore qualifier/full context or reject clip; compare source, update edit/transcript and document reason. (8)
17. Stop, record version boundary, separate outputs, pin version if possible, rerun affected jobs consistently and compare samples. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Factual integrity, consent and privacy are mandatory.
