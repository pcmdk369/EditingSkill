# CT-24 Professional Lab 01 — AI Inpainting, Upscaling and Provenance

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 4–6 hours
- Environment: Approved AI image service or local model plus raster editor; record provider/model/version
- Lab status: Authored procedure; not executed in this session

## Brief
Use a non-sensitive, learner-created image to compare conventional cleanup, AI inpainting and AI upscaling. Deliver a reviewed final with original and provenance records.

## Prerequisites
Complete CT-24 Lessons 01–05. Do not use confidential client assets or identifiable third-party images without authorization.

## Procedure
1. Define target use and acceptable change boundary.
2. Preserve original image and record dimensions/profile.
3. Review provider terms, retention, training and commercial use.
4. Create a duplicate and define a bounded mask.
5. Generate two inpainting drafts and inspect edges/details.
6. Test upscaling on a small crop before full image.
7. Compare invented detail, text, geometry and texture to source.
8. Reject unsupported or identity-altering changes.
9. Perform manual corrections on separate layers.
10. Record tool/model/version, prompt/task, settings and reviewer.
11. Export final and compare at intended display size.
12. Create manifest and disclosure note where required.

## Expected artifacts
Original, masked drafts, upscaling tests, layered final, provenance log, rights/privacy checklist and QC report.

## Validation
| Test | Method | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Compare original backup | Unchanged |
| Mask | Inspect region | Only intended area affected |
| Detail | Compare source/reference | No unsupported factual detail |
| Edges | Inspect at 100% | No obvious seams/artifacts |
| Terms | Review provider conditions | Permitted use documented |
| Provenance | Inspect log | Tool/version and edits traceable |
| Export | Open final | Correct size/profile |

## Troubleshooting
Invented text: restore verified source. Subject changed: reject and tighten mask. Upscale halo: reduce sharpening. Terms unclear: do not use externally. Batch mismatch: stop and reconcile model/version.

## Assessment — 100 points
Task selection 10; masking/inpainting 15; upscaling evaluation 15; image quality 15; source integrity 10; rights/privacy 15; provenance/disclosure 10; QC/handoff 10. Pass 80/100; rights and factual integrity are mandatory.

## Handoff
Submit source, drafts, final layers, tool/model log, rights assessment, disclosure and QC manifest.
