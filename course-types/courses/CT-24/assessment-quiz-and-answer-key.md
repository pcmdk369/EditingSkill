# CT-24 — AI-Assisted Image Editing Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. Inpainting changes: A. Selected region B. Audio C. Frame rate D. File owner.
2. Outpainting: A. Extends canvas content B. Compresses audio C. Changes copyright D. Deletes metadata.
3. AI upscaling may: A. Synthesize details B. Recover ground truth always C. Guarantee accuracy D. Establish provenance.
4. A mask controls: A. Region affected B. Model license C. Export name D. File size.
5. Provider terms should be checked for: A. Data retention and commercial use B. Only color palette C. Only resolution D. None.
6. Original files should be: A. Preserved B. Overwritten C. Deleted D. Flattened.
7. AI output may contain: A. Invented text/details B. Guaranteed accuracy C. Automatic consent D. Rights clearance.
8. Provenance records: A. Source and transformations B. Only final dimensions C. Social views D. Camera battery.
9. Human review is required for: A. Factual/identity-sensitive output B. Nothing C. Only file names D. Only export speed.
10. Portfolio permission is: A. Separate from delivery permission B. Always automatic C. Never needed D. Created by model.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Distinguish inpainting and outpainting.
12. Name three checks for AI-upscaled detail.
13. What should be checked before external upload?
14. Why keep generated edits on separate layers?
15. What should an AI provenance log include?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. AI inpainting changes a product connector and invents text on a label. Describe correction and approval. (8)
17. A client asks to upload unreleased product imagery to a provider with unclear retention terms. What should the editor do? (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. Inpainting changes a selected area; outpainting extends beyond original boundaries. (3)
12. Geometry, text, face/product details, edges and texture artifacts. (3)
13. Rights, consent, confidentiality, retention/training, commercial use. (3)
14. It supports reversibility, comparison and provenance. (3)
15. Source, tool/model/version, task/prompt, settings, output, edits, reviewer and approval. (3)
16. Reject invented areas, restore verified source detail, compare to actual product, log model/change and obtain client approval. (8)
17. Do not upload until terms and authorization are clear; use approved local processing or defer/decline. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Rights, privacy and factual accuracy are mandatory.
