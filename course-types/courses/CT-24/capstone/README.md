# CT-24 Capstone — Responsible AI-Assisted Image Campaign

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–16 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Produce a three-image campaign using bounded AI-assisted edits. Preserve originals, document generated regions and verify product/identity/text accuracy.

## Deliverables
- creative brief and allowed-change policy;
- original source images and rights/consent record;
- tool/provider/model/version assessment;
- prompt/task and settings log;
- AI drafts and manually reviewed finals;
- layered editable projects;
- factual/identity/brand accuracy checklist;
- provenance and disclosure statement;
- QC report, delivery manifest and reflection.

## Mandatory gates
No unauthorized uploads, false product features, fabricated factual evidence or unapproved likeness/identity manipulation. Verify provider terms and preserve originals.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Task selection and brief | 10 |
| Source rights/privacy | 15 |
| Masking and edit craft | 15 |
| Factual/identity accuracy | 15 |
| Human review and corrections | 15 |
| Provenance/disclosure | 10 |
| Technical quality | 10 |
| Handoff/reflection | 10 |

Pass 80/100. Rights/privacy and factual integrity are mandatory gates.

## Review
Reviewer compares originals and finals, checks provider terms, generated regions, provenance, disclosures and package integrity. Actual learner evidence is required for validation.
