# CT-28 Professional Lab 02 — Multicamera Sync, Marker Reconciliation and Privacy Audit

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 4–5 hours
- Environment: Authorized event recording, NLE, marker sheet and rights/incident log
- Lab status: Authored procedure; no switcher or platform-specific compatibility is claimed

## Brief
Synchronize three camera recordings and a program mix, reconcile operator live markers to source timecode, and prepare a privacy-reviewed highlight list.

## Procedure
1. Confirm recording permissions and access restrictions.
2. Inventory program and isolated camera/audio files.
3. Record frame rates, durations, timecode and start offsets.
4. Synchronize sources and check beginning/middle/end for drift.
5. Import operator markers and map them to each source timeline.
6. Review context around every marker and identify false or ambiguous references.
7. Flag personal information, minors, restricted areas and unapproved audience shots.
8. Verify guest, music, sponsor and venue permissions for candidate highlights.
9. Build an approved highlight list with source IDs, timecodes and status.
10. Create review proxies or contact sheets without exposing restricted media.
11. Record incidents and escalation actions; hold affected clips from publication.
12. Export approved clips and complete QC/rights manifest.

## Validation
Sync report, marker mapping, contextual review, rights/privacy gate, approved clip list and output manifest must reconcile.

## Deliverables
Source inventory, sync table, marker map, privacy/rights review, highlight list, clip exports and QC report.

## Assessment — 100 points
Sync 20; marker mapping 15; editorial context 15; privacy/rights 20; clip quality 10; QC/handoff 20. Pass 80/100.
