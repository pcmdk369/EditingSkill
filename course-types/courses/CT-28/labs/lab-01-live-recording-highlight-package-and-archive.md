# CT-28 Professional Lab 01 — Live Recording Highlight Package and Archive

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 5–7 hours
- Environment: Recorded live session, NLE, media inspector and spreadsheet
- Lab status: Authored; no streaming platform integration is tested here

## Brief
Turn an authorized 20–30 minute recorded session into a full archive master and three short highlights. Preserve context, verify guest/music rights and deliver captions and source-linked edit notes.

## Procedure
1. Confirm event recording permission and intended distribution.
2. Inventory program recording and any isolated camera/audio files.
3. Record source durations, frame rates, audio layouts and sync offsets.
4. Watch the full recording and log potential highlights with in/out timecodes.
5. Review surrounding context and retain qualifications/responses.
6. Verify speaker names, titles and factual claims against approved sources.
7. Check guest, audience, venue, music, sponsor and graphic permissions.
8. Build three highlight edits and one full archive master.
9. Create accurate captions and correct speaker labels.
10. Apply audio leveling and verify sync.
11. Export to current destination specifications verified from official platform documentation.
12. Watch all outputs and inspect metadata.
13. Create source-to-output manifest and retention/access plan.

## Validation
All clips trace to source timecodes; no clip reverses meaning; rights cover intended use; captions are reviewed; outputs play and match specification; archive includes originals or agreed masters.

## Deliverables
Archive master, three clips, captions, source log, rights/approval register, QC report and manifest.

## Assessment — 100 points
Source/sync 15; editorial context 20; highlight quality 15; audio/captions 15; rights/privacy 15; technical delivery 10; archive/handoff 10. Pass 80/100.
