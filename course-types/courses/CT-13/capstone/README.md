# CT-13 Capstone — Professional Short-Form Audio Post Package

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–16 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Create a complete audio post-production package for a 2–3 minute video, podcast segment or interview scene. The mix must meet a supplied delivery specification and remain editable/reproducible.

## Deliverables
- source inventory and rights log;
- organized DAW session;
- dialogue edit and cleanup;
- ambience/music/effects design;
- final stereo mix and requested stems;
- loudness/true-peak report;
- sync and channel-map validation;
- export metadata and QC report;
- session dependency/backup manifest;
- reflection and portfolio permission status.

## Mandatory gates
Use authorized recordings and licensed audio. Obtain consent for synthetic voice. Measure final rendered outputs and preserve source media.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Source/session organization | 10 |
| Editing and continuity | 15 |
| Cleanup and tonal balance | 15 |
| Mix dynamics and clarity | 15 |
| Sync/channel integrity | 10 |
| Loudness/export compliance | 15 |
| Rights and provenance | 5 |
| Handoff/QC/reflection | 15 |

Pass 80/100. Rights, sync and delivery compliance are mandatory gates.

## Review
Reviewer listens to full outputs, measures final files, checks stems/session and validates rights/dependencies. Actual learner artifacts and review evidence are required.
