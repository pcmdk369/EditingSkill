# CT-10 — Product & E-commerce Image Editing Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. SKU identifies: A. Product/variant B. Font C. Frame rate D. Audio channel.
2. A hero image is: A. Primary product image B. A proxy C. A watermark D. A transcript.
3. Alpha channel provides: A. Transparency B. Audio sync C. Copyright D. Resolution.
4. Product color should be matched against: A. Reliable reference B. Random screen C. Saturation preset D. Social filter.
5. A contact shadow helps: A. Ground product in scene B. Change SKU C. Add text D. Remove license.
6. Batch export should begin with: A. Test subset B. Entire catalog with no review C. Delete originals D. Rename randomly.
7. A product composite may mislead if it: A. Invents features B. Uses a clean background C. Has correct scale D. Shows actual detail.
8. SKU manifest should include: A. Variant and filename mapping B. Music tempo C. Font size only D. Editor username only.
9. AI-generated product details are automatically accurate. A. True B. False.
10. A catalog handoff should include: A. Export manifest and rights record B. Only thumbnails C. No project notes D. No approvals.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Why is variant mapping essential?
12. Name three difficult edge/material cases.
13. What can cause product color mismatch?
14. Why should lifestyle images preserve honest scale?
15. What should happen when packaging changes after a shoot?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. A batch exports 20 images but maps two color variants to the wrong SKU suffix. Explain how to detect, correct and prevent recurrence. (8)
17. AI background generation changes the shape of a product connector. Describe the correct response and evidence record. (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 B; 10 A.
11. Buyers must see the exact product variant they are purchasing. (3)
12. Glass, reflective metal, transparent packaging, soft fabric/fibers. (3)
13. Mixed light, profiles, display conditions and source reference. (3)
14. Misleading scale changes buyer expectations. (3)
15. Mark old assets superseded, arrange updated photography and replace active listings. (3)
16. Reconcile against SKU master, halt delivery, correct naming/map, regenerate affected files, run duplicate/missing checks and add preflight validation. (8)
17. Reject generated alteration, restore verified source detail, record tool/model and edit, compare to actual product and obtain approval. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Wrong product/variant representation requires correction.
