# CT-06 — Music Video & Performance Editing Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. A bar/measure is: A. A grouped set of beats B. A video transition C. A codec D. A camera angle.
2. A waveform transient: A. Always equals a beat B. Is a rapid energy change C. Is a lyric license D. Is a color value.
3. Sync should be checked: A. Only at start B. At multiple points across recording C. Only after export D. Only by guessing.
4. A multicamera cut should support: A. Random angle changes B. Performance and story C. Maximum cuts per second D. Only camera resolution.
5. A match cut links shots through: A. Similarity of movement/shape/action B. Same file size C. Different music rights D. Audio compression.
6. Speed ramping may cause: A. No risk B. Motion artifacts and altered performance meaning C. Automatic license D. Better sync always.
7. Music video rights may include: A. Only the camera operator B. Composition and sound recording separately C. Only the editor D. No rights.
8. Lyric text should be: A. Guessed B. Accurate and properly authorized C. Automatically public domain D. Hidden.
9. AI voice imitation of a real performer requires: A. No permission B. Explicit authorization and appropriate review C. Only a prompt D. No disclosure ever.
10. Delivery should identify: A. Approved audio version and usage restrictions B. Only title C. Only export date D. Only thumbnail.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Explain beat, bar and phrase.
12. Name two methods to synchronize camera recordings.
13. Why should visible instrument action be checked against audio?
14. List three distinct rights that may apply to a music video.
15. What should be included in the final handoff?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. Three camera recordings align at the beginning but drift by the final chorus. Explain diagnosis and safe correction. (8)
17. An AI tool generates a recognizable singer likeness for a transition. No written permission exists. What should the editor do? (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 B; 3 B; 4 B; 5 A; 6 B; 7 B; 8 B; 9 B; 10 A.
11. Beat is pulse, bar groups beats, phrase is a larger musical unit. (3)
12. Clap/slate, timecode, verified waveform, visual sync event. (3)
13. It protects performance credibility and avoids visibly false action. (3)
14. Composition, sound recording, performer/likeness, footage, artwork, lyrics. (3)
15. Approved master, exports, project/dependencies, rights/cue sheet, QC, version manifest. (3)
16. Check source frame rates, variable frame rate, recorder clocks and duration; inspect middle sync; use approved conform/stretch correction only when justified; verify throughout after correction. (8)
17. Do not use or publish it; obtain explicit rights/consent and review disclosure, or replace with a non-identifying authorized visual. Record the decision. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Unauthorized music/likeness use requires remediation.
