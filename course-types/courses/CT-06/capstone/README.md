# CT-06 Capstone — Music Video / Live Performance Edit

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–16 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and independent review not evidenced

## Brief
Create a 60–120 second performance edit using an approved audio master and at least three camera angles or an equivalent documented visual coverage plan. Establish a visual concept that responds to song structure and performance emotion.

## Deliverables
- creative brief and song-section map;
- source/camera inventory and sync report;
- approved audio identity/version;
- rights register for composition, recording, performers, footage, artwork and lyrics;
- shot map and visual motif sheet;
- editable project;
- final export and one platform adaptation;
- lyric/caption file if required;
- QC report, version manifest and portfolio reflection.

## Mandatory gates
No unlicensed music, unauthorized likeness/voice imitation, fabricated live performance or misleading instrument action. Confirm performer and music rights for the intended territory, platform and term.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Music structure and creative concept | 15 |
| Sync and performance continuity | 20 |
| Shot selection and visual storytelling | 15 |
| Rhythm, match cuts and effects | 15 |
| Rights, consent and provenance | 15 |
| Technical quality and delivery | 10 |
| Documentation and reflection | 10 |

Pass: 80/100. Rights and sync are mandatory gates.

## Review protocol
Reviewer checks audio identity, sync throughout, visible performance accuracy, rights records, full playback and package integrity. Capstone validation requires actual learner artifacts and documented review.
