# CT-15 — Sound Design & Foley Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. Foley is: A. Performed sound matched to picture B. Only dialogue C. A video codec D. A color LUT.
2. Ambience establishes: A. Environment/perspective B. File ownership C. Resolution D. Caption timing.
3. A transient is: A. Rapid sound onset B. A long fade C. A license D. A stereo bus.
4. Contact Foley should be: A. Synchronized to visible action B. Random C. Always louder D. Always stereo.
5. Repeating ambience loops can: A. Reveal repetition B. Grant rights C. Improve sync automatically D. Fix dialogue.
6. Perspective may be shaped by: A. Level/EQ/reverb B. Filename only C. Export size D. Font.
7. Sound library metadata should include: A. Source and license B. Only duration C. Only title D. No creator.
8. AI-generated sound should be: A. Reviewed and rights-checked B. Assumed safe C. Never logged D. Treated as factual recording.
9. Stems should: A. Share common start time B. Have random offsets C. Use different durations D. Be unnamed.
10. Voice imitation requires: A. Explicit authorization B. No permission C. Only a prompt D. No disclosure.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Distinguish Foley, ambience and designed effects.
12. Name three factors that communicate sound perspective.
13. Why should the editor preserve raw recordings?
14. What belongs in a sound-library asset record?
15. Why should stems be checked by recombination?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. A Foley footstep is late, the ambience loop repeats audibly and the final stems do not align. Explain diagnosis and correction. (8)
17. A sound library asset has no clear commercial license. What should happen before use? (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. Foley is performed action sound; ambience is environmental bed; designed effect is created/processed for narrative purpose. (3)
12. Level, frequency filtering, reverberation, stereo position and transient detail. (3)
13. It preserves source and allows re-editing/processing. (3)
14. Creator, source, license, restrictions, technical metadata and tags. (3)
15. To verify timing, routing and completeness. (3)
16. Align transient to visible contact; vary/crossfade ambience; export stems from common timeline start and reimport to verify. (8)
17. Quarantine/avoid use until rights are clarified; obtain license or replace with authorized recording; log source. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Rights and synchronization are mandatory.
