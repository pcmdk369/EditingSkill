# CT-15 Capstone — Short-Scene Sound Design and Foley Package

- Level: Master / Professional Practice
- Estimated time: 10–16 hours
- Status: Brief authored; learner execution and review not evidenced

## Brief
Design sound for a 2–3 minute scene. Use original/authorized Foley, ambience and designed effects to establish perspective, action and emotional tone while preserving dialogue clarity where present.

## Deliverables
- spotting list and sonic concept;
- source/rights register;
- raw Foley and recording notes;
- layered ambience/effects session;
- full mix;
- dialogue/Foley/ambience/effects/music stems as specified;
- common-start sync validation;
- loudness/peak report if required;
- editable session and dependency manifest;
- QC report, reflection and portfolio permission status.

## Mandatory gates
All sound assets must be authorized. Synthetic voice/likeness requires explicit permission. Sound design must not materially misrepresent real events in factual contexts.

## Rubric — 100 points
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Story spotting and sonic concept | 10 |
| Foley craft and sync | 20 |
| Ambience and perspective | 15 |
| Designed effects and restraint | 15 |
| Mix clarity and spatial coherence | 10 |
| Rights/provenance | 10 |
| Stems and technical validation | 10 |
| Documentation/handoff | 10 |

Pass: 80/100. Rights, sync and delivery integrity are mandatory.

## Review
Reviewer watches/listens to full scene, checks cue sync, perspective, mix, rights and stem recombination. Actual learner files and review evidence are required for validation.
