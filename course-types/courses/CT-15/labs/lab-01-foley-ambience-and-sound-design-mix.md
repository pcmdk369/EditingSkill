# CT-15 Professional Lab 01 — Foley, Ambience and Sound Design Mix

- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 4–6 hours
- Environment: DAW/audio editor, recorder/microphone and authorized sound assets; record versions
- Lab status: Authored procedure; not executed in this session

## Brief
Design sound for a 60-second silent scene using Foley, ambience and designed effects. Deliver a synchronized full mix and aligned stems.

## Prerequisites
Complete CT-15 Lessons 01–05. Use learner-created recordings or sound assets with documented licenses.

## Procedure
1. Watch scene without sound and create a spotting list.
2. Identify production sound that should remain and gaps requiring design.
3. Record or select authorized Foley for footsteps and prop actions.
4. Record room tone and ambience layers.
5. Label all takes with source, action, surface, perspective and take.
6. Sync Foley to visible actions.
7. Build ambience with appropriate distance and transitions.
8. Design impacts/transitions only where story supports them.
9. Balance levels and frequency space; protect dialogue if present.
10. Check stereo and mono compatibility.
11. Export full mix and stems from a common timeline start.
12. Reimport/play stems and verify alignment.
13. Complete rights, provenance and QC records.

## Expected artifacts
Spotting list, raw Foley, edited Foley, ambience/effects tracks, full mix, stems, session, asset register and QC report.

## Validation
| Test | Method | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Foley sync | Compare contact to picture | Convincing alignment |
| Perspective | Listen across scene | Distance/location coherent |
| Looping | Listen for repetition | No obvious loop artifacts |
| Mix | Full playback | No masking/clipping |
| Rights | Audit asset register | All assets authorized |
| Stems | Recombine at common start | Reconstructs full mix |

## Troubleshooting
Late Foley: align transient to action. Repeating ambience: vary/crossfade. Masked dialogue: rebalance. Stem offset: export from common start. Unknown license: quarantine asset.

## Assessment — 100 points
Spotting/story intent 10; Foley performance/sync 20; ambience/perspective 15; effects craft 15; mix clarity 10; rights/provenance 10; stems/technical QC 10; documentation 10. Pass 80/100; rights and sync are mandatory gates.

## Handoff
Submit project, full mix, stems, raw/processed Foley, asset register, cue sheet and QC report.
