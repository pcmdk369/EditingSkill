# CT-15 Professional Lab 02 — Foley Perspective, Layering and Sound-Library Provenance

- Course: CT-15 — Sound Design & Foley
- Level: Advanced / Professional
- Estimated time: 4–5 hours
- Environment: DAW/audio editor, microphone/recorder, headphones and asset database or spreadsheet
- Lab status: Authored procedure; recording equipment and room acoustics are not tested here

## Brief
Design sound for a 60-second silent visual sequence. Record original Foley for at least three actions, create an ambience bed, design one transition and deliver separated stems with complete source records.

## Learning objectives
Record repeatable Foley performances, match perspective and timing, layer sounds without masking, maintain headroom, and document ownership and reuse rights.

## Procedure
1. Review the visual sequence and create a spotting sheet with timecodes and perspective.
2. Identify sounds that can be recorded safely using ordinary objects; do not create hazardous effects.
3. Record room tone and clean Foley takes; note microphone, distance, room and performance.
4. Capture multiple variations and slate each take for identification.
5. Select takes against picture and align transient/action timing.
6. Build perspective using level, EQ, reverb and stereo placement; avoid arbitrary effects.
7. Layer transient, body and tail components where useful.
8. Maintain headroom and check phase/mono compatibility.
9. Build ambience separately from foreground Foley.
10. Create separate Foley, ambience and designed-effects stems.
11. Re-import stems at common start and verify alignment.
12. Record asset origin, creator, date, license and any restrictions.
13. Export and listen through complete sequence; check sync and clipping.
14. Package DAW session, original recordings, stems, spotting sheet and manifest.

## Validation
| Test | Method | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Sync | Compare action/timecode | Foley matches visible event |
| Perspective | Listen across shots | Space and distance feel coherent |
| Layering | Solo and full mix | No masking or unintended peaks |
| Mono | Fold down | No damaging phase cancellation |
| Stems | Re-import and sum | Alignment and documented behavior |
| Provenance | Audit asset log | Every sound traceable and authorized |

## Deliverables
Spotting sheet, original Foley takes, edited DAW session, Foley/ambience/effects stems, sound library entries, QC report and manifest.

## Assessment — 100 points
Spotting 10; recording quality 15; performance/sync 15; perspective/layering 20; mix/QC 15; provenance/rights 15; handoff 10. Pass 80/100; unauthorized assets are a mandatory failure.

## Completion checklist
- [ ] Recording setup and takes logged.
- [ ] Foley timing checked against picture.
- [ ] Perspective and ambience coherent.
- [ ] Stems aligned and tested.
- [ ] Mono/phase and peak checks complete.
- [ ] Asset provenance and rights documented.
- [ ] Session and manifest delivered.
