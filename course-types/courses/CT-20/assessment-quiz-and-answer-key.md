# CT-20 — Visual Effects & Compositing Assessment

**Total:** 50 points | **Suggested time:** 45–60 minutes

## Part A — Multiple choice (10 × 2 = 20)

1. Compositing combines: A. Image elements B. Only audio C. Fonts D. Frame rates.
2. Alpha represents: A. Transparency B. Sample rate C. Color temperature D. File size.
3. Rotoscoping creates: A. Animated masks B. Audio stems C. 3D models only D. LUTs.
4. Spill suppression reduces: A. Reflected screen color B. Motion blur C. Frame count D. Noise floor.
5. Tracking estimates: A. Image motion B. Copyright C. Loudness D. Font size.
6. Matchmove recreates: A. Camera motion B. Voice tone C. Color profile D. Music beat.
7. Render passes are: A. Separate rendered components B. Caption files C. Camera batteries D. Legal forms.
8. A matte should be reviewed: A. Across full shot B. Only one frame C. Only final still D. Without source.
9. AI cleanup may: A. Invent/alter details B. Guarantee fidelity C. Grant consent D. Remove all risk.
10. VFX handoff includes: A. Frame range, color/alpha and dependencies B. Only screenshot C. No source D. Random names.

## Part B — Short answer (5 × 3 = 15)

11. Explain straight vs premultiplied alpha.
12. Name three causes of poor keying.
13. What can cause tracking drift?
14. Why should a CG element match grain and motion blur?
15. What belongs in a VFX shot manifest?

## Part C — Applied case (15)

16. A keyed subject has green fringe, holes in the matte and tracking drift. Explain correction and validation. (8)
17. A final comp has a dark halo after export. Describe alpha diagnosis. (7)

## Answer key
1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.
11. Straight stores RGB independent of alpha; premultiplied RGB is multiplied by alpha. (3)
12. Uneven lighting, poor screen separation, motion blur, compression, spill. (3)
13. Feature loss, blur, occlusion, parallax or bad solve. (3)
14. It matches the plate's optical characteristics and avoids pasted-on appearance. (3)
15. Shot ID, frame range/handles, resolution/fps, color/alpha, source/project/dependencies/version. (3)
16. Refine key/mattes, use holdout/garbage masks, adjust spill, retrack or correct points, review full sequence and render proof. (8)
17. Check straight/premultiplied interpretation, edge RGB, color transform and export settings; test on contrasting backgrounds and rerender. (7)

## Performance bands
45–50 exemplary; 40–44 competent; 35–39 developing; below 35 remediation. Rights and temporal integrity are mandatory.
