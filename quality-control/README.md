# Quality Control Framework

## Technical QC
- File exists, opens and plays.
- Duration, dimensions, aspect ratio and frame rate match the brief.
- Codec, container, bitrate/quality and audio properties are correct.
- No missing frames, corruption, unintended black frames or frozen sections.
- Audio is synchronized, intelligible and free of unintended clipping.
- Captions are accurate, timed and readable.
- Color, exposure and graphics are consistent.
- Alpha/transparency and channel layouts are correct where required.

## Creative QC
- Message and story are clear.
- Opening engages without misrepresenting content.
- Pacing supports the intended audience.
- Cuts and transitions are motivated.
- Visual hierarchy and typography are readable.
- Sound supports rather than distracts.
- Branding and style follow the brief.

## Delivery QC
- Correct filename and version.
- Required formats and variants included.
- Source/project files included only as agreed.
- Rights, releases and attributions documented.
- Delivery manifest and archive are complete.

Record each check as Pass, Fail, Not Applicable, or Not Tested. Never mark an unchecked item as passed.
