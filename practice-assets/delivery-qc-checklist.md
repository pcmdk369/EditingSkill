# Delivery QC Checklist

Use this checklist only against an actual rendered file and project package. Mark each item **Pass**, **Fail**, **Not applicable**, or **Not tested**. Add evidence and a reviewer for every critical check.

## 1. Brief and picture
- [ ] Runtime matches the approved brief.
- [ ] Aspect ratio, frame size and frame rate match the delivery specification.
- [ ] Opening communicates the subject and purpose.
- [ ] Story order is clear; no unintended jumps or missing actions.
- [ ] No offline media, black frames, flash frames or accidental gaps.
- [ ] Titles and captions are inside safe areas and readable on target devices.
- [ ] Color and exposure are consistent; no unexpected clipping or banding.

## 2. Audio
- [ ] Dialogue is intelligible and synchronized.
- [ ] No unintended clicks, pops, clipping or abrupt edits.
- [ ] Music and effects are licensed or otherwise cleared for the intended use.
- [ ] Loudness and true-peak targets are taken from the actual delivery specification.
- [ ] Channel layout and sample rate match the delivery specification.

## 3. Captions and accessibility
- [ ] Caption text matches the approved transcript.
- [ ] Timing, reading speed, line length and speaker identification are checked.
- [ ] Punctuation and localization have been reviewed by a competent reader.
- [ ] Important information is not conveyed by color or sound alone.

## 4. Rights, privacy and AI
- [ ] Every source asset has a recorded origin and permission scope.
- [ ] Required releases, licenses and attribution are documented.
- [ ] Personal or sensitive information is removed or authorized.
- [ ] AI-generated or altered content is disclosed where required by the brief or platform.
- [ ] A human reviewer approved factual claims and final creative decisions.

## 5. Export and package
- [ ] Export preset, codec, bitrate, frame rate, resolution and audio settings are recorded.
- [ ] The rendered file was played from beginning to end.
- [ ] File opens in a second player or independent inspection tool.
- [ ] Project file, linked media, fonts and dependencies are collected where permitted.
- [ ] File names, folder structure and version labels are unambiguous.
- [ ] Checksums are generated from the final files and verified after copying.
- [ ] Delivery notes list known limitations and excluded items.
