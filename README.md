# Editing Skill™

**Universal All-in-One Editing & Creative Production Master Course**

A modular, tool-independent curriculum for learning and executing professional creative production from complete beginner through master level.

## Scope

This repository contains the full course architecture for:

- Video and film editing
- Image and photo editing
- Audio and sound production
- 2D/3D animation
- Motion graphics and typography
- Visual effects and compositing
- AI-assisted creative production
- Professional studio and post-production operations

## Learning pathway

| Stage | Level | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 00 | Foundation | Understand digital media and editing fundamentals |
| 01 | Beginner | Complete basic editing projects |
| 02 | Intermediate | Build structured multi-track productions |
| 03 | Advanced | Apply professional post-production techniques |
| 04 | Expert | Deliver specialized commercial and narrative work |
| 05 | Master | Direct complex creative pipelines |
| 06 | AI & Automation | Design human-supervised automated workflows |
| 07 | Professional Studio | Operate end-to-end production and delivery |

## Repository map

- `SKILL.md` — universal AI-agent operating instructions
- `curriculum/` — staged course syllabi and learning outcomes
- `course-types/` — complete catalog of standalone editing courses and specialization bundles
- `departments/` — specialist discipline curricula
- `software/` — application-specific learning tracks
- `workflows/` — production recipes and delivery pipelines
- `assessments/` — quizzes, practical rubrics and capstone requirements
- `templates/` — reusable production documents
- `quality-control/` — technical and creative validation standards
- `references/` — shared theory and technical references

## Course types

The course catalog covers video, film, short-form, documentary, commercial, photo, illustration, retouching, product imagery, color grading, audio, podcast, sound design, music, 2D/3D animation, motion graphics, VFX, compositing, green screen, 3D modeling, subtitles/localization, AI image/video/voice workflows, automation, live content, mobile editing, and professional finishing. Each course follows the eight-stage pathway and uses practical assessments.

## Teaching standard

Every lesson should include definition, purpose, prerequisites, theory, tools, procedure, worked example, practice, common errors, assessment, project and quality check.

## Agent safety and reliability

The agent must distinguish recommendations from executed actions, preserve original media, avoid destructive operations without approval, respect copyright and licensing, and verify exported files before reporting completion.

## Status

Master architecture and initial course framework. Lesson content and software-specific instructions are expanded progressively and should be versioned independently.
