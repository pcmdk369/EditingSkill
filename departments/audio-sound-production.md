# Department: Audio & Sound Production

## Foundation
- Waveforms, frequency, amplitude, sample rate and bit depth
- Mono, stereo, channel layouts, containers and codecs
- Gain, peak, RMS, LUFS and clipping concepts

## Beginner
- Import, trim, fade, normalize and arrange audio
- Basic dialogue cleanup and music placement
- Crossfades and simple level automation

## Intermediate
- EQ, compression, gating, de-essing and noise reduction
- Dialogue editing, sync, ambience and Foley
- Podcast and voiceover workflows

## Advanced
- Sound design, layering, spatial placement and automation
- Bus routing, dynamics, mastering chains and loudness targets
- Multitrack and surround/immersive concepts

## Expert and Master
- Narrative soundscapes, film post-production and mix translation
- Delivery specifications, stems, M&E and archival masters
- Critical listening, monitoring and room considerations

## Projects
Clean voiceover, podcast episode, short-film soundscape, commercial mix, final stems package.

## Assessment
Intelligibility, noise, clipping, balance, dynamics, synchronization, loudness compliance and format integrity.
