# Department: VFX & Compositing

## Foundation
- Layers, alpha, premultiplication and compositing concepts
- Coordinate systems, lens distortion and color management
- Plate preparation and reference gathering

## Beginner
- Simple keying, masks and cleanup
- Basic green-screen composite
- Layer order, blend modes and edge refinement

## Intermediate
- Rotoscoping, planar/point tracking and stabilization
- Screen replacement, object removal and paint
- Shadow, light wrap and integration

## Advanced
- Multi-pass compositing, CGI integration and matchmove concepts
- Particle and simulation integration
- Grain, lens effects, motion blur and final polish

## Expert and Master
- Shot breakdown, VFX planning and bid/complexity estimation
- Sequence continuity, color pipeline and finishing
- Review, versioning and delivery for VFX-heavy projects

## Assessment
Track stability, edge quality, perspective, lighting, color, grain, motion, realism and render integrity.
