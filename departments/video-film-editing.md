# Department: Video & Film Editing

## Foundation
- Video production lifecycle and editor responsibilities
- Shot sizes, camera angles, movement, coverage and continuity
- Timeline, timecode, frame rate, resolution, codecs and proxies
- Ingest, bins, selects, sync maps and project organization

## Beginner
- Assembly edits, trimming, transitions, titles and music
- Basic interview and montage construction
- Simple pacing, continuity and export

## Intermediate
- J/L cuts, match cuts, B-roll, multi-track editing
- Speed changes, keyframes, stabilization and reframing
- Dialogue synchronization and captions

## Advanced
- Narrative structure, scene rhythm and advanced continuity
- Multi-camera editing, color matching and sound design
- Trailer, commercial, music video and documentary techniques

## Expert and Master
- Feature and short-film editorial strategy
- Offline/online, conform, finishing and mastering
- Complex sequences, director/editor collaboration and delivery management

## Practical portfolio
1. 30-second montage
2. 60-second vertical social edit
3. Interview with B-roll and captions
4. Product commercial
5. Narrative scene with continuity
6. Documentary short or film capstone

## Assessment
Narrative clarity, continuity, pacing, shot selection, audio, visual consistency, technical compliance and project organization.
