# Department: Image & Photo Editing

## Foundation
- Raster vs vector, pixels, resolution, color modes and file formats
- Layers, selections, masks, paths and non-destructive editing
- Exposure, white balance, contrast, histogram and color basics

## Beginner
- Crop, straighten, resize and export
- Basic retouching, adjustment layers and text
- Background removal and simple composites

## Intermediate
- Advanced selections, luminosity masks and blending modes
- Portrait retouching, product cleanup and perspective correction
- Batch processing, RAW development and color consistency

## Advanced
- Frequency separation concepts, dodge and burn, compositing
- Image restoration, complex masking and realistic shadows
- Print vs web color workflows and proofing

## Expert and Master
- Art direction, visual consistency, advanced composites
- Editorial, campaign, fashion and product image systems
- Reproducible asset pipelines and delivery standards

## Projects
Portrait retouch, e-commerce product image, poster composite, restoration, campaign image set.

## Assessment
Natural results, edge quality, consistent lighting, color accuracy, resolution, export correctness and editable source organization.
