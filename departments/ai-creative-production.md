# Department: AI Creative Production

## Foundation
- Generative AI concepts, model limits and prompt structure
- Source rights, consent, privacy, disclosure and provenance
- Human review and factual/visual verification

## Beginner
- Prompted image/video concepts and reference planning
- Transcription, caption drafting and translation review
- AI-assisted cleanup with before/after comparison

## Intermediate
- Scene analysis, shot selection and rough-cut assistance
- Voice workflows with consent and disclosure
- Upscaling, denoising, background/object tools and artifact review

## Advanced
- Multi-step creative pipelines, batch processing and APIs
- Prompt iteration, reference consistency and asset tracking
- FFmpeg integration and repeatable automation

## Expert and Master
- Agent orchestration, tool contracts and approval gates
- Evaluation datasets, output testing and failure handling
- Cost, latency, privacy and model selection
- Human-led creative direction and final sign-off

## Assessment
Reproducibility, rights, quality improvement, artifact detection, transparency, safety, and verified delivery.
