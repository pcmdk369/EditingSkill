# Specialist Departments

Each department contains cross-stage pathways. Begin with the relevant foundation modules, then progress through practice, specialization, and capstone work.

1. **Video & Film Editing** — editorial language, timeline craft, narrative, continuity, finishing.
2. **Image & Photo Editing** — raster/vector fundamentals, retouching, compositing, image restoration, product and editorial imagery.
3. **Audio & Sound Production** — dialogue, music, sound effects, recording, mixing, mastering and delivery.
4. **Animation** — principles of animation, 2D/3D workflows, rigging, keyframes, character and object motion.
5. **Motion Graphics & Design** — typography, graphic systems, kinetic design, titles, explainers and data visualization.
6. **VFX & Compositing** — keying, rotoscoping, tracking, cleanup, CGI integration and finishing.
7. **AI Creative Production** — generative media, AI-assisted editing, speech workflows, automation, provenance and human QA.

## Department lesson requirements
Every specialist course must state prerequisites, learning outcomes, terminology, tools and alternatives, workflow, practice assets, assignment, evaluation rubric, export requirements, and known limitations.
