# Department: Animation

## Foundation
- Timing, spacing, squash and stretch, anticipation, follow-through
- Keyframes, interpolation, frame rate and motion paths
- 2D vs 3D animation concepts

## Beginner
- Simple object motion, transforms and loops
- Basic character poses and bouncing-ball exercise
- Easing and readable movement

## Intermediate
- Walk cycles, graph editors, parenting and constraints
- 2D rigging, 3D rigging concepts and camera animation
- Scene layout, lighting and rendering basics

## Advanced
- Character performance, lip sync and secondary motion
- 3D materials, lighting, simulations and render optimization
- Animation compositing and motion polish

## Expert and Master
- Visual development, shot design and animation direction
- Production planning, asset reuse and pipeline coordination
- Multi-shot sequence and final animation capstone

## Assessment
Timing, spacing, weight, silhouette, performance, continuity, technical cleanliness and render quality.
