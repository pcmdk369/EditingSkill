# Department: Motion Graphics & Design

## Foundation
- Composition, hierarchy, contrast, alignment and visual systems
- Typography, grids, color and design principles
- Keyframes, transforms and animation curves

## Beginner
- Animated titles, lower thirds and simple logo reveals
- Basic transitions and text animation
- Export with alpha where supported

## Intermediate
- Kinetic typography, shape layers and infographic animation
- Parent-child systems, expressions and reusable templates
- Rhythm, easing and synchronized motion

## Advanced
- Data-driven motion, complex compositions and 3D layers
- Brand systems, explainers and broadcast packages
- Responsive layouts and multi-format adaptation

## Expert and Master
- Art direction, motion language and design systems
- Complex title sequences and campaign packages
- Template architecture and scalable production

## Assessment
Legibility, hierarchy, timing, consistency, brand alignment, accessibility and export quality.
