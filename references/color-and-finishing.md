# Color Science and Finishing

## Correction vs grading
Correction aims for technically consistent exposure, white balance and shot matching. Grading establishes a deliberate creative look.

## Workflow
1. Confirm source color space and project color management.
2. Balance exposure and white balance.
3. Match shots using scopes and visual comparison.
4. Apply creative look.
5. Check skin tones, highlights, shadows and saturation.
6. Review on an appropriate display and in context.
7. Export and inspect for shifts or clipping.

## Concepts
Understand gamut, transfer functions, gamma, log footage, LUTs, waveform, RGB parade, vectorscope, legal/video levels, HDR and SDR. Avoid applying LUTs blindly; confirm input and output transforms.

## Finishing
Check titles, safe areas, captions, fades, black frames, audio peaks, sync, color consistency, frame rate and export integrity.
