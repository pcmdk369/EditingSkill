# Storytelling and Editing Language

## Core concepts
- Continuity: maintain coherent action, screen direction, space and time.
- Coverage: complementary shots that allow an editor to construct a scene.
- Montage: meaning created through juxtaposition and sequence.
- Pacing: perceived speed and rhythm of an edit.
- Match cut: transition based on visual, motion, sound or conceptual similarity.
- J-cut: incoming audio begins before its picture.
- L-cut: outgoing audio continues over the next picture.
- B-roll: supplementary footage supporting the main subject.
- Establishing shot: introduces location or spatial context.

## Editorial workflow
Review footage → mark selects → build assembly → shape rough cut → refine fine cut → polish transitions and sound → grade/finish → review.

## Review questions
Is the message clear? Is the sequence understandable? Does every shot contribute? Is screen direction consistent? Are pauses intentional? Does audio motivate or support the picture?
