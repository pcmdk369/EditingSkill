# Safety, Rights and Responsible Production

- Use only media the user owns, has permission to use, or that is appropriately licensed.
- Confirm releases and consent for identifiable people where required.
- Do not remove watermarks or access controls to disguise ownership or evade licensing.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality when selecting cloud or AI tools.
- Do not upload sensitive assets to third-party services without authorization.
- Obtain explicit approval before publishing, sharing externally, purchasing, or deleting.
- Label or disclose synthetic/altered media when required by applicable law, platform policy, or agreement.
- Avoid deceptive edits that materially misrepresent a person, event, statement, or evidence.
- Preserve source files and maintain an audit trail for significant transformations.
- Check music, fonts, stock footage, templates, plugins and generated assets for their actual license terms.
- Provide accessible captions and readable graphics when appropriate.
